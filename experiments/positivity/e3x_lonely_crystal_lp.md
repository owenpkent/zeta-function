## 1. Object

The LCC cone ([first_principles_conjecture_program.md](../../docs/03_research/first_principles_conjecture_program.md) 3.1, #76; transported form [lcc_bc_transport.md](../../docs/03_research/lcc_bc_transport.md), #82): pairs $(\mu, c)$ with $\mu \ge 0$ tempered, $c_n \ge 0$, satisfying for all even $g \in C_c^\infty$

$$\int \hat g\,d\mu = \hat g(\tfrac i2) + \hat g(-\tfrac i2) - 2\sum_{n\ge1} c_n g(\log n) + \frac{1}{2\pi}\int \hat g(r)\,\Omega(r)\,dr,$$

$\Omega(r) = \mathrm{Re}\,\psi(\tfrac14 + \tfrac{ir}2) - \log\pi$; pole term $= 2\int g(x)\cosh(x/2)\,dx$. D-H analogue: no pole, $\Omega_{DH}(r) = \log 5 + \mathrm{Re}\,\psi(\tfrac34 + \tfrac{ir}2) - \log\pi$, $\Omega_{DH}(0) = -0.6212$ (density $-0.0989$, the "$-0.099$" of #76).

**Discretization.** $\mu \to$ nonnegative weights on a uniform $r$-grid ($[0,R]$ step $h$, coarse extension to $3R$ step $0.5$ absorbing tail mass); comb variables $c_n$, $n \le N = e^L$. Tests: plain Fejer triangles ($\hat g \ge 0$, $g \ge 0$), cosine-modulated triangles ($\hat g \ge 0$), translated triangle pairs; all with closed-form $\hat g$ and pole. Equalities carry slacks $|s_j| \le \varepsilon_j$ with $\varepsilon_j$ = arch-truncation tail + beyond-grid spectral allowance (Riemann-von Mangoldt density, tolerance only) + grid-quantization term ($\propto h^2$) + $10^{-3}$ scale.

**Semantics (load-bearing).** Grid restricts the cone, finite tests relax it: primal values are INDICATIVE, the deliverable of (b)/(e) is the refinement TREND. Dual/Farkas certificates with sign conditions valid on all of $\mathbb{R}$ are rigorous in the continuum; run (c)'s single-triangle certificate has its sign conditions in closed form. Guard: run (a) must pass on the same grid before (b)/(c)/(e) are read.

**(c) D-H discipline: INFEASIBLE at every rung, certificate extracted.** Phase-1 infeasibility gap $t^* = 0.233 \to 0.370$, growing under refinement.
