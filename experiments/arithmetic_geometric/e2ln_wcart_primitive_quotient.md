# 2LN: does Petrov non-semisimplicity survive the primitive quotient?

> Companion for [`e2ln_wcart_primitive_quotient.py`](e2ln_wcart_primitive_quotient.py).
> Run: `python -m experiments.arithmetic_geometric.e2ln_wcart_primitive_quotient`.
> Result artifact: [`e2ln_wcart_primitive_quotient.npz`](e2ln_wcart_primitive_quotient.npz).

## Question

This is the only WCart direct-polarization escape hatch left after 2LL (#67) and
2MM (#68). Petrov (arXiv:2302.11389, Annals) proved the WCart Sen operator is
**not semisimple**. The sharp strategic question:

> Can Petrov non-semisimplicity be harmless after passing to the primitive
> quotient, or does any nontrivial nilpotent surviving on primitive `H^1`
> obstruct a positive Hodge-Riemann polarization?

Two prior coordinates frame it:

- **2LL (#67)**: the Euler-Sen monodromy form `Q_N(x,y)=Omega(x,N y)` survives the
  first cheap kill (escapes the 2KK hyperbolic diagonal-star trap).
- **2MM (#68)**: but that form is a transport theorem, `signature(Q_N)=signature(B)`,
  so it manufactures no positivity.

2LN is the next test: a defective Sen block kills the **direct** Hermitian
metric, but does the **primitive quotient** rescue it?

## Method

A faithful defective Sen model is assembled from `sl2` irreps (single Jordan
blocks, fixing the 2KK defect where the "Jordan block" collapsed to a relabeled
diagonal):

```text
N      = lowering operator (ones on the subdiagonal), genuine Jordan block
Theta  = -1/2 I + N                          (Sen operator at the Tate center)
Omega  : N^T Omega + Omega N = 0             (the cup form; Theta^T Omega + Omega Theta = -Omega)
W(N)   = monodromy weight filtration generated by N
P_k    = primitive part of Gr^W_k = ker(N^{k+1} : Gr_k -> Gr_{-k-2})
Q_k    = c_E * Omega(x, N^k y)               (the HR primitive form; c_E = +/-1 the Weil phase)
```

The four parts:

1. **Part 1 (the 2LM direct metric).** Test feasibility of the direct Hermitian
   Rosati/Lyapunov metric `Theta^* H + H Theta = -H, H > 0`. With
   `A = Theta + 1/2 I = N`, a positive `H` exists iff `A` is similar to a
   skew-Hermitian matrix, i.e. diagonalizable with purely imaginary spectrum.
2. **Part 2 (Jacobson-Morozov erasure).** Measure the same-weight (endomorphism)
   action of `N` on the primitive graded quotient, and test the Lyapunov metric
   on the (semisimple) quotient operator.
3. **Part 3 (the residual gap).** Recompute `Q_k` with the two Weil phases
   `c_E = +1` and `c_E = -1` and read both signatures.
4. **Part 4 (Outcome-3 control).** Take a misaligned nilpotent `L` (a different
   `sl2`, random basis) and measure how much `N` still acts inside `ker L`.

Audit A-E: sign convention, transpose vs conjugate-transpose, derivation
nullspace dimension vs the Tate-dual Jordan matching count `sum_{a,b} min(m_a,m_b)`,
basis invariance under random conjugation, and the D-H discipline (formation
guard plus an injected off-line zero at `beta ~ 0.8085` giving polarization
defect `|1 - 2 beta| ~ 0.617`).

## Verdict

**Outcome 1, sharpened: the Petrov nilpotent DIES on the primitive quotient, so
non-semisimplicity is NOT the wall.**

The mechanism is Jacobson-Morozov / the `sl2` theorem: the monodromy weight
filtration that `N` itself generates makes `N` a pure Lefschetz lowering operator
(weight `k -> k-2`), hence the **zero endomorphism** on every primitive graded
quotient. The direct Hermitian metric fails on the full nonsemisimple block
(Part 1, the 2LM failure), but it exists on the semisimple primitive quotient
(Part 2). So passing to the primitive quotient buys the right formal shape and
erases the nilpotent for free.

**But the primitive quotient buys NO positivity.** The obstruction relocates,
undiminished, to the **sign** of the primitive monodromy form `Q_k`: Part 3 shows
`Q_k` is `+definite` or `-definite` depending only on the `C_E` Weil-operator
phase, with identical `|defect|`. That choosable sign is governed by the
archimedean Hodge/Frobenius datum, not by `N`. It is exactly the unmoved M4 /
arithmetic Hodge-standard-conjecture gap.

**The missing datum is the Lefschetz operator, not `Theta`.** Part 4 shows that
with a misaligned (non-canonical) operator the nilpotent survives on the
"primitive" subspace (`||N|ker|| ~ 0.29` vs `0` for the canonical `N`-aligned
choice). So the primitive decomposition that erases the nilpotent must come from
the correct geometric Frobenius/Lefschetz operator. That operator is the open
construction.

**Net:** WCart stays alive. The direct WCart Hodge-Riemann route is not closed by
Petrov non-semisimplicity. The next task is geometric: construct the
Frobenius/Lefschetz operator that supplies the primitive decomposition and fixes
the sign of `Q_k`. This is the same M4 burden as #68, now stated on the primitive
quotient: build `C_E` (the archimedean Weil operator) geometrically and prove it
is a positive compatible complex structure on each primitive piece, without
zero-side or RH input.

## Discipline Status

**K1.** The finite algebra does not discharge K1. The erasure of `N` is
structural (Jacobson-Morozov) and free, but the polarization SIGN is supplied as
a phase `c_E`, not derived. Importing `c_E` (or `B`) from a Weil form or known
zeros would be circular. A future proof must construct the archimedean `C_E` as a
geometric datum.

**K2.** The formation guard reads `L.has_euler_product` (data-based, aligned with
2MM and the Lean `FrobeniusAlgebra` guard); D-H and a renamed D-H both fail to
form. The injected off-line zero reproduces the 2HH/2JJ (#61/#63) polarization
defect `0.617`: on the line the cup is a polarization (defect `0`), off the line
it is not.

**Petrov.** This is the first probe to genuinely exercise the Petrov
non-semisimplicity crux that 2KK only named: a real defective Jordan block, the
monodromy weight filtration it generates, and the primitive forms `Omega(x, N^k y)`.
The conclusion is that the crux is **routable** (the nilpotent dies on the
primitive quotient) but not **helpful** (the positivity gap is unchanged).

**Scope.** Finite linear algebra on a faithful `sl2`/Jordan model, not absolute
prismatic cohomology. Proves nothing about RH. It resolves the strategic question
(Petrov nilpotent dies on primitive, by Jacobson-Morozov) and relocates the gap
to the primitive-form sign / the geometric Lefschetz operator. WCart remains
live, but only through a real geometric primitive projector, not through
`C`-linear algebra alone.
