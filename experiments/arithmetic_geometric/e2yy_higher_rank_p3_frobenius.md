## The question

e2xx (#123) sharpened the AHK gap: the moment sequence $\{t_n\}$ = the curve's zeta $P(T)$ is
generated by one Frobenius endomorphism $\pi$ (char poly $P(T)$), so "carry $P(T)$" = "supply
$\pi$, a degree-preserving self-map with $|\mathrm{eig}(\pi)|=\sqrt q$". Does a matroid Chow ring
**contain** such a $\pi$ as a genuine graded-ring endomorphism?

## The answer (a near-theorem, adversary-supplied)

A degree-preserving **graded-ring** endomorphism $T$ of $A^1$ must respect the Chow
multiplication $A^1\times A^1\to A^2$, which on $A^1=\mathrm{span}(e_1,p_1)$ of the $(1,2,1)$ ring
is the **Lorentzian** form $Q=\mathrm{diag}(1,-1)$ ($e_1^2=e_2$, $p_1^2=-e_2$; the Hodge-index
$(1,n{-}1)$, e2g/#48). So $T$ must be a **similitude** of $Q$: $T^{\!\top}QT=\mu Q$. The
similitudes of an indefinite binary form are exactly:

| similitude of $Q=\mathrm{diag}(1,-1)$ | eigenvalues | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| diagonal $\mathrm{diag}(\lambda,1/\lambda)$, $\lambda^4=1$ | roots of unity, $\|\mathrm{eig}\|=1$ | automorphisms; the $q=1$ shadow (#40) |
| hyperbolic **boost** | **real**, unequal | the **off-line/non-RH** Frobenius ($t^2>4q$), #119 |
| **scalar** $\lambda\cdot\mathrm{Id}$ | $\|\mathrm{eig}\|=\|\lambda\|$ | forced to the **supersingular boundary** $t=\pm2g\sqrt q$ |

The **generic interior Frobenius** $\pi$ (RH, $|t|<2g\sqrt q$) has eigenvalues $\sqrt q\,u,
\sqrt q\,\bar u$ with $|u|=1$ -- a scaled **rotation**. A rotation preserves the **definite** form
$\mathrm{diag}(1,1)$ (verified: $\mu=q$), **not** the indefinite Chow product $\mathrm{diag}(1,-1)$
(verified: no scalar $\mu$). **So the on-circle Frobenius is not a graded-ring endomorphism of the
lattice at all.** This is #119 (real-root combinatorial vs complex-root Frobenius) **as a
near-theorem**, sharper than an eigenvalue-modulus taxonomy.

## The deepest phrasing

A matroid Chow ring is purely **even/algebraic (Tate type)** -- the analogue of $H^{2*}$, every
class in weight $2k$, all Frobenius eigenvalues powers of $q$ (here the trivial $q^0=1$ on $A^1$).
The modulus-$\sqrt q$ (half-integer-weight) Frobenius eigenvalues live in **odd, weight-1
cohomology $H^1$** (a symplectic similitude), which a Chow ring **does not have**. Putting the
$H^1$ Frobenius onto $A^1$ (weight-2, NS-like, symmetric Lorentzian) is a category mismatch -- and
that mismatch **is** why the lattice cannot supply $\pi$. "P3 = supply the arithmetic Frobenius" =
"supply the $H^1$ the Tate-type combinatorial ring lacks". This is the honest content of #40,
sharpened.

## The one genuinely new wrinkle (adversary-confirmed sound)

The scalar (scaling) self-map reaches $|\mathrm{eig}|=\sqrt q$ but, being scalar ($\alpha=q/\alpha$)
under a real moment sequence, lands **exactly** on the degenerate supersingular boundary
$t=\pm2g\sqrt q$ ($c_1=\pm2g$). So even the one native operator that hits modulus $\sqrt q$ is
pinned to the RH-extremal point, never the interior. This closes the "but a scaling reaches
$\sqrt q$" loophole. Everything else is #40 + #119 + e2xx at the operator level.
