## 2. The derivation: the off-diagonal funding need made explicit (Task 1)

**Setup.** Montgomery normalization, matching the AF scaling: even test function $\psi$ with $\hat\psi$ supported in $[-\Delta, \Delta]$; support $\Delta = 1$ is the pair-correlation boundary; prime sums run to $X = T^\Delta$. Funding split per the at-source verification ([`af_funding_inputs_verification.md`](../../docs/03_research/reading_notes/af_funding_inputs_verification.md) Finding 5, corrected here per adversary Fix 2): the trace $\mathrm{tr}\,\tilde G$ is funded ZERO-side (their Prop 4.2: Gabor frame + Riemann-von Mangoldt count; no prime sum enters the trace); the prime side funds only the HS budget $\|\tilde G\|_{HS}^2$ (their §5, proven in-house as their Theorem 5.7). The HS entry's prime side is, schematically after unsmoothing their Gabor window to a $t$-average,

$$M(X, T) \;=\; \frac{1}{T}\int_T^{2T} \Big| \sum_{n \le X} \frac{\Lambda(n)}{n^{1/2 + it}}\, \phi\!\Big(\frac{\log n}{\log X}\Big) \Big|^2 dt \;=\; \underbrace{\sum_{n \le X} \frac{\Lambda(n)^2}{n}\phi^2}_{\text{diagonal}} \;+\; \underbrace{\sum_{m \ne n \le X} \frac{\Lambda(n)\Lambda(m)}{\sqrt{nm}}\,\phi\phi\, K_T(\log\tfrac{n}{m})}_{\text{off-diagonal}},$$

with $K_T(u) = \frac{1}{T}\int_T^{2T} e^{iut} dt$, so $|K_T(u)| \le \min(1,\, 2/(T|u|))$: a kernel concentrated at $|u| \lesssim 1/T$. The AF Gabor window changes the $O(1)$ kernel shape, not the scalings below; the $H(\delta)$ and weight-profile bookkeeping is kernel-generic (stated scope).

**The diagonal, any support (KNOWN).** $\sum_{n \le X} \Lambda(n)^2/n = \tfrac{1}{2}\log^2 X + O(\log X)$: Mertens/PNT-class, funded unconditionally at every $\Delta$. This is the AF note's "the prime side computes the spectral statistics unconditionally" clause.

**(a) $\Delta \le 1$: the off-diagonal is controlled unconditionally, and the mechanism is the classical mean-value range (KNOWN; cite, not re-prove).** For distinct integers $n < m \le X$: $\log(m/n) \ge 1/m \ge 1/X \ge 1/T$. The Montgomery-Vaughan mean value theorem (the generalized Hilbert inequality, Montgomery-Vaughan 1974; used in exactly this role in Montgomery 1973's $F(\alpha)$ computation for $\alpha \le 1$, and in the Aryan / BGSTB evaluations AF credits as lineage: AF re-proves its budget in-house as Theorem 5.7, and its Appendix A claims full coverage with no hypotheses on the main theorems' types, per [`af_funding_inputs_verification.md`](../../docs/03_research/reading_notes/af_funding_inputs_verification.md) Finding 1 and Section 5) gives

$$\int_0^T \Big|\sum_n a_n n^{it}\Big|^2 dt = \sum_n |a_n|^2 \big(T + O(n)\big),$$

so with $a_n = \Lambda(n) n^{-1/2}\phi$ the entire off-diagonal is priced at $O(\sum_{n \le X} \Lambda(n)^2) = O(X \log X)$, negligible against the diagonal's $T\log^2 X$ exactly while $X \lesssim T$: support $< 1$ free, support 1 the boundary. Two typing remarks. (i) The separation input $|\log(n/m)| \ge 1/X$ is itself an additive-lattice fact (integers are 1-separated). (ii) But at this support the funding is DENSITY-CLASS in the operative sense: a density-matched Beurling system satisfies PNT-class and mean-value-class facts under its own axioms with no FE and no lattice (the verified Revesz/Broucke line documented in [`beurling.py`](../_shared/beurling.py): "everything provable from Beurling density/counting data is FE- and lattice-agnostic"). The deliberation's sentence "the unconditional funding ends at support 1 because that is where the proven mean values end" is exactly this. The wall F1a types is strictly beyond.

**(b) $\Delta = 1 + \delta$: the binding new input is the prime-pair family at integer shifts.** Past $X \asymp T$ the MV price $X\log X$ exceeds the target $T \log^2 X$ (the AF paper's own ceiling wording: "the off-diagonal prime sum is no longer dominated by the diagonal"), so the off-diagonal must be EVALUATED with cancellation, not bounded. Substitute $m = n + h$, $h \ge 1$ (negative $h$ symmetric): $\log(m/n) = h/n + O(h^2/n^2)$, and the kernel confines $h/n \lesssim 1/T$, i.e.

$$1 \le h \le H(\delta), \qquad H(\delta) \asymp \frac{X}{T} = T^{\delta} = X^{\delta/(1+\delta)} = X^{\,1 - 1/\Delta}.$$

For each $h$ the kernel is order 1 on $n \in [hT, X]$ and oscillates the range $n < hT$ away ($|K_T| \le 2n/(Th)$), so by partial summation in $n$,

$$\mathrm{OD} \;=\; \sum_{h = 1}^{H(\delta)} \int_{\max(hT,\,2)}^{X} (\text{kernel weight})\, \frac{dB_x(h)}{x} \;+\; (\text{mirror}), \qquad B_x(h) = \sum_{n \le x} \Lambda(n)\Lambda(n + h):$$

the off-diagonal is a functional of the family $\{B_x(h) : 1 \le h \le H(\delta),\ x \le X\}$ ALONE. That family is the data class. The weight each $h$ enters at:

$$w_\delta(h) \;=\; \int_{hT}^{X} \frac{dx}{x}\cdot O(1) \;=\; \log\frac{X}{hT} + O(1) \;=\; \log\frac{H}{h} + O(1),$$
