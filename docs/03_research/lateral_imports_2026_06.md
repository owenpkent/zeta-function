## The pattern the scan exposes

Every live import is a **uniqueness/rigidity technology**, not a positivity detector. That is consistent with the program's state: the cone-is-a-singleton statement (LCC/EFR composite pinching) is the named target, detectors are frozen, and the missing mathematics is "what forces uniqueness when the spectrum has a continuous part." Three known technology families can do uniqueness-with-continuum:

1. **Group-measure rigidity** (scaling quasi-invariance + ergodicity): proven in BC, executed as e2nn, transport target stated in #81.
2. **Inverse spectral uniqueness** (canonical systems / Krein): proven for measures with ac parts; the Euler-structure question is open and expensive.
3. **Crystalline-measure structure theory** (ACV/Kurasov-Sarnak): currently discrete-only; the continuous extension is an external WATCH item.

Flat extension (the F_q mechanism, #79) is the fourth family and is proven NOT to transfer (#80). The scan's verdict: spend the next lateral effort on transporting (1), keep (2) as a queued BUILDER question, watch (3).
