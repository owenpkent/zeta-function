## 2. The two parked specs (G4, G15)

Both specs are parked; they are not scheduled work, and running either is an ORCHESTRATOR decision subject to the funding-boundary frame's exit 3a (an unscheduled measurement trips the bright line).

**G4 (the Euler-constrained SDP majorant).** Ansatz: $P = \sum_{p \le P^*} c_p B_p + c_\infty B_\infty$ with $B_p$ prime-sourced blocks (rank-1 modes at the $\{k \log p\}$ nodes, $\Lambda$-weighted) and $B_\infty$ the archimedean block, coefficients free, solved with cvxpy (CLARABEL) for $P \succeq B_{\mathrm{win}}$ where $B_{\mathrm{win}}$ is e2bh's window Weil matrix. Score against e2az's trilemma (D1 primitive-positive / D2 K1-clean / D3 Euler contact): this is the first candidate that could in principle hold D2 and D3 at once. Any run must pre-register three outcomes first: (a) infeasible on the D-H window (the discipline's pass condition), (b) feasible on zeta windows with margin decaying along #180's $e^{-\gamma_1^2\sigma^2}$ law (the expected wall re-measurement), (c) feasible on D-H (the kill: the ansatz consumes only density and is disqualified). The matrices already exist in the e2bh npz.

**G15 (Beurling descent, the rigidity meter).** Parametrize the fake by $\varepsilon_p$, loss $=$ theta-FE defect (the e2ak meter) $+ \lambda \cdot$ integer-count drift, and descend from three pre-registered starts: the default fake ($\varepsilon = 0.25$), a random re-seed, and e2bi's $D = 4$ commensurable snap. The pre-registered fork: the optimum either snaps to $\varepsilon \equiv 0$ (the integers are an isolated minimum: lattice rigidity measured as a basin) or stalls at a non-lattice critical point with a measurable defect floor (a fake that locally passes the lattice meters, the more informative outcome). Either resolution is a datum on the e2bc ramp's geometry, run backwards.
