# The construct gallery: ways to build the missing object, including wrong ones

**Date**: 2026-08-26. **Status**: catalog + three executed builds (G1/G2/G3 below).
**Charter**: user-directed session ("make a list of possible ways to build the construct even if wrong, and build some"). The construct is the missing object of [`missing_object_interface.md`](missing_object_interface.md): the five-component datum $X = (H, \mathrm{Fr}, (B, \Delta), \mathrm{TF}, \mathrm{pol})$ whose full inhabitant proves RH, open at exactly two joints (C1 = SP2 $\wedge$ SP3, C2 = SP5 = M4).

## 0. Why build wrong constructs on purpose

The project's standing result (#201) is that the wall is one statement seen from many sides: uniform-in-cutoff funding on the vanishing locus. A deliberately wrong build is useful exactly when it converts a qualitative prohibition into a measured coordinate: where does the wrongness localize, what did the shortcut consume, and what is the closed-form price. Every entry below therefore carries a **predicted failure mode with a citation**; a build whose failure lands where predicted is a re-measurement of the wall (per the #201 derivability check, so labeled), and a build whose failure lands elsewhere would be news. The disciplines are the referees: a form-side build that also works for Davenport-Heilbronn is disqualified; a counting-side build that also works for the Beurling fake is disqualified.

## 2. What the three executed builds measured

Full numbers in the dossiers; one paragraph each here.

**G1 (e2bh, the surgery costume).** The zero-side compressed Weil form on a 21-Gaussian window family at $t \in [80, 90]$: zeta's matrix is PSD at the numerical floor (surgery is a no-op: free exactly where information-free, the #170 clause in matrix coordinates), D-H's has exactly one negative eigenvalue (the off-line pair at unit index weight, the #202 mechanism datum reproduced in a 21-dim window), and the deleted eigenvector's line profile peaks at the off-line landmark $\gamma \approx 85.7$: the forced polarization cannot be written down without reading the zeros, which is the K1 sin quantified. The surgery then outputs a PSD form for D-H, so the discipline disqualifies the route on contact. Labeled: re-measurement of the wall (K1 face), new costume.

**G2 (e2bj, the mollifier costume).** Truncated Li coefficients: zeta's stay nonnegative for every $n$ (on-line terms are pointwise $\ge 0$), D-H's go negative at a measured $n^*$, and the tuned Gaussian damping $\Gamma^*(N)$ that buys $\lambda_n \ge 0$ up to horizon $N$ exists for every finite $N$, decreases as the horizon grows, and every purchased horizon expires (the same $\Gamma$ fails at a measured larger $n$). Positivity is purchasable at every finite horizon and never uniformly: the uniformity clause of M4 in the cheapest instrument in the repo. Labeled: re-measurement of the wall (uniformity face), new costume.

**G3 (e2bi, the function-field costume).** Snapping $\{\log p\}$ to $\tfrac1D\mathbb{Z}$ makes the truncated Euler product exactly periodic with period $2\pi D$ (Frobenius structure exists literally, verified to machine precision, and is absent for the true primes), while the max snap jitter is $\tfrac1{2D}$: the product (jitter bound) $\times$ (structure height) $= \pi$ **exactly, independent of $D$**. To see the imported F_q structure below zeta's first zero ($\gamma_1 = 14.13$) one must accept jitter at the scale of the default Beurling fake ($\varepsilon = 0.25$); to be faithful to arithmetic one pushes the structure above every fixed height. The additive-lattice cost is measured on the same ladder (integer-count drift vs $D$). Labeled: re-measurement of the wall (the commensurability face of #162/#188), with one new closed-form constant ($\pi$) for the trade.

## 3. Honest close

Nothing in this session moved the frontier, by design: the charter was to enumerate and to build wrong things whose failures are informative. The gallery's value is (i) the catalog itself, with every route's failure mode pinned to a citation, so no future session rebuilds a costume unknowingly; (ii) three cheap standing instruments (a K1-localization meter, a horizon-price meter, a commensurability trade meter) that any future SP5 candidate can be dropped into; (iii) two LISTED builds with genuine open content (G4's Euler-constrained SDP, G15's Beurling descent), which are the gallery's forward edge.
