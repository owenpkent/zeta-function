## 1. The Gap: From Spectrum to Signature

Experiment 2R yielded the dynamical spectrum (the orbit lengths $\{\log p\}$ and $- \zeta'/\zeta$). The M3 Analytic Domination break proved that the strict Euler-product test ($M_{\mathrm{euler}} = A_{\mathrm{arch}} + P_{\mathrm{pp}} + B_{\mathrm{pole}} \ge 0$) isolates the stealth window and detects RH via arithmetic geometry. 

However, a spectrum is Level 3 (trace formula). RH is Level 4 (positivity / signature). We need a mechanism that forces the eigenvalues of the flow generator to lie on $\mathrm{Re}(s)=1/2$, which requires a **positive-definite inner product** (a signature) on the primitive cohomology.

## 2. Deninger's Section 6 Mechanism

In *Motivic L-functions and regularized determinants II* (Section 6), Deninger specifies the exact cohomological mechanism that generates this signature:
1. **The Alternating Form:** The product surface carries a perfect, alternating cup-product pairing on its $H^1$:
   $$ \langle -, - \rangle: H^1 \times H^1 \to H^2 \cong \mathbb{C}(-1) $$
2. **The Derivation:** The flow generator $\Theta$ is a **derivation** with respect to the cup product (the Leibniz rule):
   $$ \Theta \langle h, h' \rangle = \langle \Theta h, h' \rangle + \langle h, \Theta h' \rangle $$
3. **The Skew-Adjoint Shift:** Because $H^2 \cong \mathbb{C}(-1)$, $\Theta$ acts on the target as multiplication by $-1$. Therefore:
   $$ -\langle h, h' \rangle = \langle \Theta h, h' \rangle + \langle h, \Theta h' \rangle \implies \langle (\Theta - \tfrac{1}{2}I)h, h' \rangle + \langle h, (\Theta - \tfrac{1}{2}I)h' \rangle = 0 $$
   This makes the shifted operator $\Theta - \frac{1}{2}I$ **skew-adjoint**.
4. **The Consequence:** Skew-adjointness on an alternating form forces the generalized eigenspaces (the zeros) to have even multiplicities and forces the global root number to be $+1$. To force $\mathrm{Re}(s) = 1/2$, this alternating form must be polarized into a positive-definite symmetric form via a Hodge-$\star$ operator.

## 4. The Hodge-$\star$ Polarization and the M3 Bound

To turn the alternating pairing $\langle -, - \rangle$ into a symmetric signature (a positivity statement), we define the polarized form:
$$ Q(x, y) = \langle x, \star y \rangle $$
where $\star$ is the Hodge star operator. The positivity of $Q$ on the primitive cohomology is exactly the Hodge Index Theorem.

### The Arakelov Green's Function as the Metric
The Hodge-$\star$ requires a choice of metric (a polarization). On an arithmetic surface, the Arakelov geometry dictates that this metric is provided by the **Green's function at the archimedean place**. 
- In the numerical experiments (2I, 2L, M2.5), we isolated this metric contribution as the Bombieri-form archimedean block, $A_{\mathrm{arch}}$.
- The operator $\star$ geometrically represents the twist by the Arakelov metric.

### Constructing the Trace Form
When we evaluate the trace of the polarized form $Q(x, y)$ over a basis of test functions $\Phi_{b_i}$ (acting as sections of the cohomology), we recover the arithmetic Weil form. The structural components map precisely:
1. **The Polarization Metric:** $\mathrm{Tr}(\star) \mapsto A_{\mathrm{arch}}$. The archimedean block provides the positive-definite metric background.
2. **The Flow Action:** The action of the global Frobenius correspondence $\Gamma_S$ (which is infinitesimally generated by $\Theta$) on the finite primes yields the intersection numbers. Because $\Gamma_S$ operates prime-by-prime (the $p$-adic completion stricture in prismatic theory), its support is strictly on prime powers. This yields the block $P_{\mathrm{pp}}$.
3. **The Hyperbolic Direction:** The fundamental class / diagonal contributes the rank-1 positive direction $B_{\mathrm{pole}}$.

Therefore, the trace of the geometrically polarized cup product is exactly the matrix analyzed in the M3 experiment:
$$ M_{\mathrm{euler}} = A_{\mathrm{arch}} + P_{\mathrm{pp}} + B_{\mathrm{pole}} $$

### The Stealth Window: a Candidate Explanation (conjectural)
The M3 experiment demonstrated, numerically, that $M_{\mathrm{euler}}$ is negative for Davenport-Heilbronn and positive for $\zeta$ (and for Epstein up to height 120).

The proposed geometric reading: Davenport-Heilbronn lacks an Euler product, so its coefficients delocalize onto composite numbers ($P_{\mathrm{comp}} \neq 0$). IF the $p$-adic stricture required for a well-defined $\Gamma_S$ correspondence on the absolute prismatic site forces $P_{\mathrm{comp}} = 0$ on the geometric side, then the D-H "false positivity" seen in earlier truncations is the non-geometric compensation term $P_{\mathrm{comp}}$ propping up the Gram matrix.

That conditional is exactly the open problem. Deleting $P_{\mathrm{comp}}$ by hand (as M3 does) is the same as assuming the Euler-product geometry; it is NOT yet shown that $M_{\mathrm{euler}}$ is the canonical, uncorrupted geometric trace of the Hodge-$\star$ polarized pairing. The M3 experiment supplies a discriminator and a sharp target. The identification of that discriminator with a cohomological trace is the content still to be proved (§5).

## 5. Conclusion

The M3 result (the positivity of $M_{\mathrm{euler}}$ for $\zeta$ and Epstein, its negativity for D-H) is a candidate numerical trace of a Hodge-index signature on the product surface. The reading proposed here, that evaluating the trace over the primitive cohomology kills the composite cross-terms ($P_{\mathrm{comp}} = 0$) via the $p$-adic completion stricture of absolute prismatic cohomology, is a conjecture, not a recovered bound.

The structural proof of RH along this route would require proving three things, all OPEN:
1. that the canonical prismatic filtration forces $P_{\mathrm{comp}} = 0$ on the geometric side (this is what would license the hand-deletion in M3);
2. that the cup-product pairing on $H_{\mathrm{prism}}(\mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z} \times_{\mathbb{F}_1} \mathrm{Spec}\,\mathbb{Z})$ is perfectly alternating;
3. that its polarization by the Arakelov $\star$-operator yields a metric whose signature is $(1, \rho - 1)$, identically recovering $M_{\mathrm{euler}} \ge 0$.

M3 sharpened the target and supplied a discriminator that passes the Davenport-Heilbronn discipline. It did not establish any of 1-3. The honest status is: a concrete, well-posed conjecture with a numerical witness, not a proof.
