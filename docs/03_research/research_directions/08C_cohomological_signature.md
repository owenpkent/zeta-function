## 2. Deninger's Section 6 Mechanism

In *Motivic L-functions and regularized determinants II* (Section 6), Deninger specifies the exact cohomological mechanism that generates this signature:
1. **The Alternating Form:** The product surface carries a perfect, alternating cup-product pairing on its $H^1$:
   $$ \langle -, - \rangle: H^1 \times H^1 \to H^2 \cong \mathbb{C}(-1) $$
2. **The Derivation:** The flow generator $\Theta$ is a **derivation** with respect to the cup product (the Leibniz rule):
   $$ \Theta \langle h, h' \rangle = \langle \Theta h, h' \rangle + \langle h, \Theta h' \rangle $$
3. **The Skew-Adjoint Shift:** Because $H^2 \cong \mathbb{C}(-1)$, $\Theta$ acts on the target as multiplication by $-1$. Therefore:
   $$ -\langle h, h' \rangle = \langle \Theta h, h' \rangle + \langle h, \Theta h' \rangle \implies \langle (\Theta - \tfrac{1}{2}I)h, h' \rangle + \langle h, (\Theta - \tfrac{1}{2}I)h' \rangle = 0 $$
   This makes the shifted operator $\Theta - \frac{1}{2}I$ **skew-adjoint**.
4. **The Consequence:** Skew-adjointness on an alternating form forces the generalized eigenspaces (the zeros) to have even multiplicities and forces the global root number to be $+1$. To force $\mathrm{Re}(s) = 1/2$, this alternating form must be polarized into a positive-definite symmetric form via a Hodge-$\star$ operator.
