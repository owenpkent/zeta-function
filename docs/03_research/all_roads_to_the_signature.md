# All roads lead to the signature: cross-direction synthesis (2026-05-30)

> A consolidation written after a session that proposed five new directions (9-13) and pushed the THH route (Direction 10) far enough to hit, via Hesselholt's theorem and a literature survey, the same wall every other architecture hits. The finding is structural and worth stating plainly.

## The one-sentence finding

Every architecture and every new direction produces the **realization** of $\zeta$ (a determinant, a trace, a detector, a Dirichlet-series identity), and that half is comparatively easy and exists in several frameworks; but RH itself is the **signature / positivity**, which is separate, irreducible, and the same problem in every framework. The realization is not the proof. The signature is.

## Why this is the marginal-positivity thesis, restated

The project's central structural finding, "RH is just barely true, no buffer for soft proofs", is exactly the statement that the signature is the whole content. If the positivity had any slack, a soft realization (a trace, a statistic, a determinant) would close it. It has zero slack (#18: D-H fails Weil positivity by 78.7% per off-line direction; the wall is tight), so the realization frameworks, which see only the trace/spectral side, cannot close it. The session added three independent confirmations from new bases (log-concavity #27, Jensen/Turán #27, and now the THH/Hesselholt route #29), each landing on the same signature.

## The cleanest evidence from this session: Hesselholt closes the loop

Hesselholt's theorem (#29) is decisive precisely because it is the strongest possible realization result: it *proves*, over $\mathbb{F}_q$, that $\zeta$ is the regularized determinant of the Frobenius flow on the $S^1$-Tate periodic $\mathrm{TP}$. And yet RH for the variety is *not* a corollary, it is $|\alpha_i| = q^{1/2}$, the Weil/Hodge-index positivity, a separate input. So even the best realization theorem in the literature confirms the dichotomy: the determinant is the easy half; the signature is RH. Over $\mathbb{Z}$, Hesselholt's framework breaks for the #25 reason (TP not periodic, no single Frobenius), so it does not even deliver the easy half there, but the lesson stands.

## The D-H discipline is the reason the signature is the right locus

The wrong-approach detector cuts the same way every time: the realization frameworks are often *buildable* for Davenport-Heilbronn (D-H has a functional equation, hence an explicit formula, hence a trace), and that is exactly why a trace-side proof is suspect (K1/R3.5). The signature is *unbuildable* for D-H: no Euler product $\Rightarrow$ no Frobenius correspondence $\Rightarrow$ no surface $\Rightarrow$ no intersection form to take the signature of (2G's K2 reading; #25/#26's bidegree form). The one object that cannot be built for the known counterexample is the one object whose positivity is RH. That is not a coincidence; it is the discipline telling us where the proof must live.

## Operational implication: a research program, not a catalog

Stop building realizations. Connes, Deninger, Hesselholt, Morin, and the project's own 2R/2Q dictionary already supply the determinant/trace side over $\mathbb{F}_q$ and partially over $\mathbb{Z}$. The signature is the work, and it is a *program with attackable milestones*, not a 1% lottery ticket. The sharpened target is **arithmetic Rosati positivity** (the standard-conjecture form; see [`research_directions/08A_rosati_standard_conjecture.md`](research_directions/08A_rosati_standard_conjecture.md)):

> RH = positivity (no negative spectrum) of the Rosati trace form $B(x,y)=\mathrm{Tr}(x\,y^\dagger)$ on the arithmetic Frobenius algebra $\mathcal{A}$ of $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z})$.

This is the actual engine of Weil 1948, needs no ambient surface to state, is the arithmetic analogue of Grothendieck's **Hodge standard conjecture**, and is non-circular (the positivity comes from a polarization, not from the zeros). The four equivalent function-field faces are all in the record: Rosati positivity (2T), primitive intersection negative-definiteness (2G), $|\alpha_i|=\sqrt q$, and the TP/flow zeros on $\mathrm{Re}(s)=1/2$ (2S).

The milestones (08A §4), with status as of 2026-05-30:
- **M1** (done, 2T): verify the function-field Rosati formulation. The full $2g$-dim Rosati trace form has no negative eigenvalues $\Leftrightarrow |\alpha_i|=\sqrt q$, across genus 1-2.
- **M2 / M2.5 / M2.6** (done, 2U/2V/2W; LEARNINGS #32-34): construct the truncated non-circular arithmetic Weil form $M = A_{\mathrm{arch}} + P_{\mathrm{fin}} + B_{\mathrm{pole}}$ from the Gamma factor + von Mangoldt primes (no zeros), with the archimedean block in its validated Bombieri physical-space form ($C_\mu = \log\pi - \psi((1+\mu)/2)$). RESULT: $\min\mathrm{eig}(M) = +0.035 > 0$ for $\zeta$ (non-circular positivity certificate, necessary-not-sufficient). BUT the four-way RH-vs-Euler discrimination FAILS: D-H reads spuriously positive ($+0.094$) because its off-line obstruction ($\gamma\approx85.7$, $\sim$2.6% of the raw spectrum) sits below the reconstruction-residual floor: the stealth window (#18/#19/#27) on the non-circular Rosati side. The infrastructure (correct non-circular Weil form for any Gamma factor) is complete and validated.
- **M3** (attempted; outcome = a validated discriminator, NOT the analytic proof, and NOT geometric progress; LEARNINGS #35-37). The attempt (3M/e3m) split $P_{\mathrm{fin}}$ into prime-power $P_{\mathrm{pp}}$ and composite $P_{\mathrm{comp}}$ and formed $M_{\mathrm{euler}} = A_{\mathrm{arch}} + P_{\mathrm{pp}} + B_{\mathrm{pole}}$ by deleting $P_{\mathrm{comp}}$. This separates the controls (zeta $+0.035$, D-H $-0.929$) and, under stress testing, holds up as a NUMERICAL discriminator: it stays positive on independent Euler products $\chi_3,\chi_4$ with $P_{\mathrm{comp}}=0$ (#35), and fires negative on a second, independently-constructed and FE-verified off-line control at conductor 7 (#36). But it is NOT the analytic domination M2.6 demanded, and it adds NO geometric content: $M_{\mathrm{euler}}(\zeta) = M(\zeta)$ already (the Euler product makes $P_{\mathrm{comp}}=0$ a theorem, not a geometric fact), and for D-H there is no surface, so $M_{\mathrm{euler}}$ is the trace of no object (#37). The "prove $P_{\mathrm{comp}}=0$ geometrically" sub-goal is struck as vacuous-or-inapplicable.
- **M4** control the $P\to\infty$ limit (= the arithmetic Hodge standard conjecture); **M5** derive RH and verify K1/K2/K3.

The numerical phase (M1-M2.6, plus the M3 discriminator) is complete: the non-circular Rosati form is built and validated, and it confirms exactly what the marginal-positivity thesis predicts -- a soft/numerical certificate is positive for $\zeta$ but the bookkeeping that discriminates $\zeta$ from D-H (deleting $P_{\mathrm{comp}}$) carries no geometric meaning on the $\zeta$ side. The frontier is unchanged from before M3: construct the surface for $\zeta$ and prove its cup-product signature (the genuine analytic/geometric domination), where #20/3M always located the hard step.

## The acoustic frame names the split, and unifies three findings (2026-06-13)

A passive-network reading of the explicit formula ([`acoustic_passive_lossless.md`](acoustic_passive_lossless.md), LEARNINGS #90-94) gives the ledger's realization/signature split a physical name and, in doing so, ties together three findings the program tracked separately. The impedance $Z=-\zeta'/\zeta=\sum\Lambda(n)n^{-s}$ is **passive** (a positive von Mangoldt comb) iff there is an Euler product: the realization (easy half) $=$ passivity, unconditional, and it is exactly what D-H lacks (its comb goes negative at $n=3$). RH is the separate statement that the medium is **lossless** (resonances on the critical line). The coupling that turns passive into lossless is **passive $+$ reciprocal (functional equation) $\Rightarrow$ lossless**, which is Weil's two-sided argument as network theory; the missing ingredient is the energy form that **halves the free passive bound** $1\to\tfrac12$, and that halving is Poincaré duality placing the self-dual middle weight at the geometric mean of the extremes ($q^{1/2}=\sqrt{q^0\cdot q^1}$). So the signature in the right column is, acoustically, "make the medium lossless," and the three pillars become one picture:

- **marginal positivity** $=$ a reciprocal passive network is *forced* lossless (no slack to spend);
- **the D-H discipline** $=$ no Euler product $\Rightarrow$ active medium $\Rightarrow$ no weight ladder $\Rightarrow$ no halving;
- **the $2/3$ analytic ceiling** $=$ the halving is geometric (a signature), so analytic methods that attempt it without the energy form stall.

The construction route (Darlington synthesis: passive $=$ lossless 2-port $+$ one resistor, the single resistor identified with the one archimedean place) lands on Suzuki's canonical system, which is RH-equivalent. So the acoustic frame is a clarifying, unifying **language** for the signature, confirming its locus from a new direction; it is not a new lever. It reinforces this document's thesis exactly: the realization is free, the signature is RH.

## Breadth over proof-engines: the fourth convergence and the sharpened AHK face (2026-06-14)

The convergence has now been tested one level deeper. The ledger above, and the cohomology scorecard below, match $\zeta$ against frameworks and theorem *statements*. A 2026-06-14 sweep (LEARNINGS #97, an 11-agent workflow) instead matched **proof engines**: the mechanism by which a theorem manufactures a $(1, n-1)$ signature. Five from-below signature-manufacturing engines were fingerprinted against the M4 spec and run through five kill-criteria (off-line-flip / indefinite-gate / euler-archimedean / fq-shadow / k1-noncircular): Adiprasito-Huh-Katz flip-induction, Lorentzian polynomials, semismall / relative Hodge-Riemann, tropical / non-archimedean Monge-Ampere (Boucksom-Jonsson), and CKS limit-mixed-Hodge-structure on the prismatic Sen operator. **All five were killed; zero bridges survived.** This is the fourth independent all-roads confirmation, after the acoustic arc (#90-94), the Lee-Yang kill (#95), and the seven-candidate crazy-idea screen (#96): the convergence is now near-theorem-strength irreducibility, and the from-below signature-manufacturing family is closed five ways.

Two coordinates sharpen the thesis rather than move it:

- **AHK is the one retained positive coordinate.** It alone passes k1-noncircular (its Hodge-Riemann signature comes from a submodular-Lefschetz flip, never read off zeros) and the function-field shadow, so it is the unique proven witness that a $(1, n-1)$ signature is manufacturable K1-clean **with no ambient variety**. It is killed only as a *finished* object: the standard Chow-ring form is $t$-blind (#40; identical $(1,3)$ for $t=2$ and $t=100$), its primitive form is positive-definite (the indefinite piece sits in a derived 2-plane, AHK Lemma 9.6), and its degree map returns an integer, not $q+1-t$. Repairing exactly those three blindnesses while keeping the K1-clean machinery is the sharpened BUILDER target [`research_directions/09A_ahk_arithmetic_lattice.md`](research_directions/09A_ahk_arithmetic_lattice.md): a finite graded prime-lattice with a $t$-carrying submodular Lefschetz element and an indefinite $(1, n-1)$ primitive form, positivity (= M4) left open. It is strictly sharper than Direction 8.A: it asks for the lattice plus the Lefschetz element, not the variety. This is the **AHK-side face** of M4; its Arakelov-side face is the Faltings-Hriljac product plus the $\Gamma_S$ archimedean place.
- **CKS / limit-MHS is killed as a transport, not a source.** The nilpotent $N$ gives the weight filtration $W(N)$ and the $\mathrm{Gr}_\ell \cong \mathrm{Gr}_{-\ell}$ duality for free, but definiteness on primitive pieces is always the nearby-fiber polarization $Q$ imported through the degeneration. Over $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z})$ there is no disk to degenerate over and no $Q$, so importing $Q$ IS M4 (already proven twice in-repo: the 2MM transport theorem; sorry-free `SenDefiniteObstruction.lean`).

The positive coordinate extracted, as a design rule: the $(1, n-1)$ form must be **sourced** from a polarization on a real arithmetic object whose intersection numbers ARE the Frobenius trace $t$, never **propagated** from a combinatorial certificate (the Lorentzian bracket, which is $t$-blind) nor **transported** through a degeneration (CKS). Breadth-over-engines is now spent and converged exactly like breadth-over-statements (6b); its honest value is as a convergence-detector, a corpus-completer (it surfaced and then killed Boucksom-Jonsson, now recorded in [`../../experiments/lemma_db/transfer_search.py`](../../experiments/lemma_db/transfer_search.py)), and a spec-sharpener. The one still-unspent axis is a regime-two frame-audit (does a distant frame collapse two M4 conjuncts at once, as the acoustic reading collapsed carries-trace into passivity).
