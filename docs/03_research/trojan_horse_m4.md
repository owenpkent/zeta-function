## 1. The question

"How can we trojan horse a solution to M4?" M4 is the arithmetic Hodge-index / Rosati polarization; in CCM coordinates it is the Section-7 uniform determinant-class limit (LEARNINGS #154/#158). A trojan horse is any construction in which the SIGN comes for free from a structure (a state, a self-adjoint operator, a convex body, a compact class), and the RH content is relocated into a clause that does not look like a positivity: an identification, a convergence mode, a growth bound, a logical meta-statement. The question is legitimate because positivity cannot be approximated into existence (the marginal-positivity compass): if M4 is ever proven, the sign will almost certainly be INHERITED from a structure rather than checked. The trojan program asks which structure, and what the inheritance costs.

## 3. The conservation law

Five costumes, two customs posts. Every executed trojan pays its tariff at either the identification/uniformity joint (M4 in its CCM Section-7 form) or the carrier joint (C1/R1); no costume has ever moved the content anywhere else. The tariff is always the same: the load-bearing clause must consume the Euler product AND the additive lattice at the same joint (the Tate package, the #152 bracket). The two disciplines are precisely the fare-dodging detectors: D-H (FE without Euler product) catches form-side dodges, Beurling (Euler product without lattice) catches counting-side dodges, and the DMV screen pre-kills any mechanism running on density data alone. So the operational form of the trojan question is not "can we avoid paying" but "at which customs post is the tariff payable with tools that actually exist". This round executed that question for the newest costume: compactness.

## 4. The compactness trojan: the three-link split and the survey

The move: trade the ONE uniform statement ($\hat\xi_\lambda \to \Xi$ in determinant class, uniformly) for three individually cheaper links: (i) certify the finite-$\lambda$ objects into a positivity class COMPACT after renormalization, so subsequential limits exist for free (Montel/Helly, no rate); (ii) a Carleman-type determinacy condition forcing all subsequential limits to coincide (a one-sided growth statement, not a positivity); (iii) the #160 pin identifying the unique limit as $\Xi$.

Survey verdicts ([`compact_class_determinacy_survey.md`](reading_notes/compact_class_determinacy_survey.md), all load-bearing citations FETCH-VERIFIED after the adversary re-fetch round):

- **Laguerre-Polya: KNOWN-TO-REPO.** Closure under locally uniform limits IS the Hurwitz frame the CCM corpus already runs; closed but not compact, and the Montel bound needed for pre-compactness is exactly the open uniform control. Zero gain.
- **Bounded-type normal families: OFF-TARGET.** Fixed type excludes both the family (types $\approx 0.93..1.00 \times \log\lambda$ diverge, #160, re-measured in e1t on all three streams) and $\Xi$ itself; rescaling to fixed type keeps only density data = DMV-killed.
- **Lorentzian cones: OFF-TARGET.** Compactness is real but degree-pinned (Branden-Huh Thm 2.25/2.28); the family's degree grows with $\lambda$, and at growing degree the class collapses into Laguerre-Polya.
- **Polya frequency / Edrei-Thoma: ADJACENT-WATCH.** Thoma-simplex compactness is genuinely free in a weaker-than-uniform topology, but the RH content relocates into mass conservation on the simplex = a two-sided weighted trace equality = the C1 joint, with no literature route.
- **Determinate moments (Carleman/Krein): glue only.** Determinacy is density-only, hence correctly weightless under the DMV screen; standalone OFF-TARGET.
- **De Branges chains / trace-normed canonical systems: NEW-LOAD-BEARING.** The one compactness that is free, type-divergence-proof, and reality-preserving: trace-normed Hamiltonians form a compact metric space with the Hamiltonian-to-Weyl-m map a homeomorphism (Remling; at-source published corroboration Hur arXiv:1501.01268, which also pins the boundary condition and the degenerate elements inside the compact target). In this encoding the RH content relocates into exactly two clauses: (a) identification of the limit chain (= the #160 pin, the lattice site) and (b) a non-degeneracy / no-mass-escape clause for the limit Hamiltonian (the Euler site).
- **The composite move is UNPOSED.** No one poses compactness + determinacy in place of uniform convergence for zeta's limits: zero hits across the CCM corpus (2511.22755 full-text re-scanned by the adversary, 2511.23257 included), de Branges's own attempts (which ASSUMED positivity axioms; Conrey-Li), Kaltenback-Woracek (no indefinite extremal theorem exists; the #168 $HB_1$ question stays unclaimed), and the sum-rule school. Killip-Simon (math-ph/0112008, verified at theorem level) is the proven template for exactly this trade in a neighboring field: sum rule + Helly compactness + one-sided semicontinuity replacing uniform control, with the catch that the support $[-2,2]$ and sign structure are INPUTS there and would be the OUTPUTS for zeta. Closest miss: the RMT canonical-systems school (Valko-Virag stochastic zeta, Hur, Painchaud), which runs the compactness half as standard technology but only for Level-3 universality objects, probabilistically identified, with no determinacy pin and no $\Xi$. Adjacent prior on the encoding side: Suzuki arXiv:1204.1827 builds a canonical system from zeta with RH equivalent to positive semidefiniteness of the Hamiltonian family (the encoding exists; the compactness trade is not posed there).
