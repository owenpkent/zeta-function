## The headline

Our margin/ground-state territory (#180, #181, gallery entry 20, e2ao/e2ap) is a NAMED, ACTIVE research line: the localization program for Weil's quadratic form, initiated by **Yoshida (1992)**, developed by **Bombieri (2000, 2003)**, made operator-theoretic by **Connes-Consani (2023)** and **Connes-Consani-Moscovici (2025+)**, and unified two days ago by **Suzuki (arXiv:2606.09096 v2, 2026-08-18)**. ## Placement, claim by claim

1. **"Margin per unit L^2 mass on a window" (#180's object).** KNOWN AS A PROGRAM. Yoshida 1992 defined exactly this Rayleigh quotient $Q_W(v)/\|v\|_{L^2}^2$ on localized spaces, proved positivity for sufficiently small windows (the theorem our "certifiable" region instantiates), proved odd-function positivity alone implies RH, and showed RH is equivalent to non-degeneracy on the completion. Bombieri 2000 proved the minimum is ATTAINED on $L^2$ windows and re-proved Yoshida's small-window positivity; his negative-eigenvalue theorem (count = half the off-line zeros, at large enough truncation) is the in-print theory behind this repo's D-H indefiniteness detectors (e3c2, e2an SP5 cells). Credit adjustment for our ledger: #180/#181 measure objects that belong to Yoshida's program.
2. **The closed-form decay law $\mathrm{margin}(\sigma) = 4\sqrt{\pi}\,\sigma\,e^{-\gamma_1^2\sigma^2}$ (#180).** NOT FOUND in the corpus. The literature's focus is qualitative (positivity thresholds, attainment, continuity in the window, operator realization); no Gaussian-family decay law or first-zero-exponent statement surfaced in the sweep. The law is elementary once $\omega^* = 0$ is known, but as a stated, tested law with the certification-price reading it appears to be ours. Keep the claim, add the Yoshida-frame credit.
3. **The zero-locking of the minimizer (#181(i)).** KNOWN IN CONJECTURAL/THEOREM FORM, EMPIRICALLY OURS. CCM proved (per Suzuki's account, [4, Thm 5.10]) that on a finite-dimensional restriction the lowest eigenfunction's Fourier transform has ONLY REAL zeros; Suzuki's central conjecture (1.2) states $c_a\hat{v}_a(z) \to \xi(1/2+iz)$ as the window grows: the ground state's spectral profile converges to the xi function itself. Our measured locking (every reachable zero gets a node to $4\times10^{-3}$, at every scale) is the empirical face of exactly this circle, obtained independently. 4. **The band-ceiling saturation (#181(ii): the multi-mode margin governed by $\Omega$, not $\sigma$).** NOT FOUND as stated. The adjacent school is Fourier optimization (Carneiro et al.: extremal band-limited functions against the explicit formula), where band-limited constraints are native; a ceiling-vs-window scaling split for the Weil-form bottom did not surface. Treat as plausibly novel, flag to the Fourier-optimization corpus before any stronger claim.
5. **The completeness-at-scale certificate (e2ap Part A).** KNOWN IN SUBSTANCE. Certified zero-verification (Turing's method; this repo's own e5f at height $5\times10^7$) is the same epistemology; our contribution is only the coordinate system (the certificate runs through the object's lattice-sourced multiplier with an explicit five-term error budget, making SP2's finite-scale completeness a property of the BUILT object). No novelty claim beyond framing.
6. **The S-finite Euler ladder (e2ap Part B).** KNOWN TERRITORY, sharpened home found: Gonek 2007 (arXiv:0704.3448) proves RH iff short truncated Euler products approximate $\zeta$ in most of the right half-strip, and GHK's hybrid product formalizes primes-and-zeros as complementary factors (the in-print cousin of the two-meter law). Our rungs (identity to $3.9\times10^{-6}$; $\gamma_1$-dip condensing $0.586 \to 0.167$ while bounded away from zero; duality failing at every finite $P$) are a finite, bracket-typed instance. Frame as instantiation, cite Gonek/GHK.
7. **The Cramer control (e2ap Part C).** NO DIRECT PRIOR INSTANCE surfaced (a jittered-integer lattice run as an RH-methods control). Neighbouring theory: the Beurling literature knows counting data underdetermines analytic structure (Broucke-Debruyne-Vindas 2020: Beurling integers with RH and large oscillation; the dual-system generalized FE line shows FE analogues force continuous dual systems). Our measured triple (residue exact 1.0005 / duality defect 0.460 / descent drift $6.9\times10^{-4}$, nine orders above zeta and three below Beurling) appears to be a new, if modest, calibration point: the descent axis has a measured THIRD position, and the pre-registered prediction (jitter degrades Poisson cancellation to $\sim e^{s}$ without destroying convergence) landed exactly.
