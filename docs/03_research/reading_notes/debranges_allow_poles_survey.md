## 1. The question, restated precisely, with its three clauses

e1w Section 11 (handed forward, verbatim question): does the positive "allow poles" extension
class (de Branges' own, per Burnol) admit an extremal/majorant theory in which the two pole
directions with near-degenerate positive coupling play the role the #163/#164 corridor wanted
the indefinite $\kappa = 1$ structure to play? Concretely, graded against three clauses:

- **(a) The space.** An EXTENSION of a de Branges space $\mathcal H(E)$ by finitely many pole
  directions: equivalently, de Branges spaces of meromorphic functions, entire-functions-with-
  poles extension classes. The model instance is Burnol's $L_a \supset K_a$ with
  $\dim(L_a/K_a) = 2$ (poles of the completed Mellin transform at $s = 0, 1$, a mirror pair
  about the structural axis, one on each side). NOT the space $\mathcal H(E)$ itself in
  meromorphic clothing (see Section 5 for exactly this trap).
- **(b) The theory.** Extremal/majorant: an optimization or one-sided approximation theory
  (admissible majorants, Beurling-Selberg majorant/minorant, $L^1(|E|^{-2}dx)$ minimization),
  not merely reproducing-kernel structure, completeness/minimality, or inverse spectral theory.
- **(c) The signature.** POSITIVE-definite (Hilbert). Pontryagin/indefinite theories are NOT an
  answer: that route is closed at source by e1w ($\kappa(L_a) = 0$; the $(1,1)$ block lives only
  at the twisted pairing whose Krein completion is non-Pontryagin), and $HB_\kappa$ was already
  swept clean by [`hb1_one_sided_extremal.md`](hb1_one_sided_extremal.md).

## 4. Clause (a) alone: the allow-poles class exists in print, with zero extremal content

The class is real and has a small, closed in-print corpus [FETCHED at the JTNB source this
session]: de Branges' Sonine spaces (1964, "Self-reciprocal functions", credited by Burnol's
Theorem 2.1 attribution), Burnol's extension $L_a$ with $\dim(L_a/K_a) = 2$ and poles at
$s = 0, 1$ (JTNB Prop. 2.2, from "On Fourier and Zeta(s)" 6.10), Burnol's CRAS notes
(2001-2003), and de Branges' own pole-tolerant "double-Sonine" constructions in the 1992/1994
program papers (per Burnol's Section 8 commentary). Everything proved on this class is
structural: completeness/minimality of evaluator systems, reproducing-kernel identities,
functional equations, zero distribution of the structure functions. Burnol's own phrase for the
class is "the de Branges spaces, and their extensions allowing poles" [FETCHED, JTNB Section 1].
The de Branges-program positivity conditions (the only positivity-flavored statements ever
posed near this class) are not an extremal theory, and Conrey-Li examined the approach and
"indicate its difficulty" [FETCHED abstract]; numerically the conditions fail for the
zeta-loaded spaces [SECONDARY]. No majorant, minorant, admissibility, or optimization statement
exists anywhere in this corpus. Nothing found cites Burnol/Sonine together with majorant or
extremal vocabulary (Q4, Q13; concurring with the bbh rung's zero-grep of BBH for
Burnol/Sonine/zeta/Riemann [REPO]).

## 5. Clauses (b) + (c) together: the positive majorant/extremal corpus never leaves the base space

**The Havin-Mashreghi line and its maximal form.** The founding theory poses on model
subspaces $K_\Theta \subset H^2$ [REPO + BBH intro, FETCHED]. Its maximal "meromorphic" form is
exactly the nearest miss: Baranov-Borichev-Havin, whose own abstract defines the class as
"$K_B = H^2 \ominus BH^2$... the space of square summable meromorphic functions with the poles
at the points $\bar z_n$" [FETCHED, two independent routes]. The poles are the Blaschke
normalization artifact: fixed, in the LOWER half-plane, at the conjugates of the zeros of $B$.
The body makes the identification explicit: for meromorphic inner $\Theta = E^*/E$,
"the mapping $F \mapsto F/E$ is a unitary operator from $\mathcal H(E)$ onto $K_{\Theta_E}$"
[FETCHED]. So "meromorphic functions with fixed poles" IS $\mathcal H(E)$ in meromorphic
clothing: codimension zero, no new directions, and no pole on the structural side. This is the
evasion-vocabulary trap the tasking pre-registered, confirmed at source: the phrase "de Branges
spaces of meromorphic functions" in the literature refers to this unitary re-dressing, not to an
extension class. The theory itself is genuinely one-sided and positive (admissibility,
$\mathrm{Adm}^\pm$ variants, the minimal-majorant dichotomy of their Theorem 2.6), which is why
it passes clauses (b) and (c) fully while failing (a) at the exact joint that matters.

**The Toeplitz-kernel reformulation.** Makarov-Poltoratski generalize the whole admissibility
program to Toeplitz kernels "acting in the Hardy space $H^2$ in the upper halfplane" [FETCHED,
API abstract]; kernels are $H^2$-internal by definition. The most general in-print form of the
admissibility question therefore still lives inside the holomorphic class. Clause (a) fails.

**The Beurling-Selberg / Carneiro-Littmann machine.** Every paper in the lineage (Gaussian
subordination through the 2024 sign-uncertainty paper and, per the adversary round, the 2025
Chirre-Helfgott Beurling-Selberg-type papers) requires the extremal functions to be
ENTIRE of exponential type and the framework space to be a de Branges space OF ENTIRE FUNCTIONS
[FETCHED: 1008.4969 and 2408.01186 abstracts at source; the API-swept Littmann list; REPO:
1406.5456 hypotheses (P1)-(P4) pinned at source by the falsifier survey]. Targets may be
singular; the extremal CLASS never is. Clause (a) fails throughout.

**The Woracek-school positive papers.** Per the tasking's instruction, the POSITIVE-space
papers of the Kaltenbäck-Woracek school were checked (their indefinite work is the NOT-key):
the majorization series and the finite-dimensional-subspaces paper are classical $HB$
throughout, with zero occurrences of meromorphic/pole vocabulary, and "finite-dimensional"
modifies the majorant-generated SUBSPACE of $\mathcal H(E)$, never an ambient extension
[REPO, hb1 + bbh rung, both at full-text tier]. Clause (a) fails.

**The 2022-2024 window.** Vasilyev's BM-majorant generalization is Paley-Wiener classical
[FETCHED]. Limani 2024 is the newest arXiv paper whose abstract contains "admissible majorant"
and it proves an ABSENCE result inside $H(b)$ [FETCHED, API sweep F1]. The full arXiv corpus
with that abstract phrase is four papers; none allows poles beyond the model-space

## 9. Why the joint slot is empty: the finite-dimensionality observation (this note's own synthesis)

Flagged as this dossier's own derivation, assembled from [FETCHED]/[REPO] ingredients; not a
claim found in any source.

1. **Monotonicity trivializes the inherited problem.** $K_a \subset L_a$ implies every
   majorant admissible for $K_a$ is admissible for $L_a$: $\mathrm{Adm}(K_a) \subseteq
   \mathrm{Adm}(L_a)$. A majorant theory on the extension that does not explicitly isolate the
   quotient is dominated by base-space theory: posed but blind, the positive-space twin of the
   fork hb1 Section 7 recorded on the indefinite side (and of the BBH winding-blindness of

   is governed by the 2-dimensional quotient (Prop. 4.5): its Gram geometry is exactly the
