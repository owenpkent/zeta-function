# Reading notes: van Frankenhuijsen, *The Riemann Hypothesis for Function Fields over a Finite Field* (arXiv:0806.0044, 2008)

> SURVEYOR deep-read dossier, 2026-07-01, commissioned by the Stepanov engine audit ([`../stepanov_engine_audit.md`](../stepanov_engine_audit.md), Section 5.6), which had this paper at abstract depth only. Read this session from the full LaTeX source (arXiv e-print): Section 1 (intro), Section 3 (the Bombieri proof), Section 4 (the Nevanlinna speculations) in full; Section 2 (Tate-thesis local/global theory, roughly half the paper) at heading depth.

## One-line takeaway

The one serious published attempt to transfer Stepanov-Bombieri to $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z})$: it splits the coordinates of $C \times C$ into a geometric one (the polynomial variable) and an arithmetic one (the coefficients), models the product as {integer-coefficient functions on the unit disc}, gets the bookkeeping layer exactly right (Nevanlinna counting function $=$ Chebyshev $\psi$; Poisson-Jensen $=$ product formula), openly identifies the missing Frobenius with the missing derivative, and then stops at the author's own admission: **"there is no diagonal and we have to force the vanishing of $f$ at the primes in an artificial manner."** The wall is R1 + PROP-global, named by the author in 2008 in his own vocabulary; no positivity, no signature, no M4 anywhere. Nobody has advanced the model since (26 citations, zero follow-ups on the proposal).

## 1. What the paper actually is

Three-quarters exposition, one-quarter proposal.

- **Section 1 (intro).** History of the function-field RH (Artin, F.K. Schmidt, Hasse, Weil, Stepanov, Bombieri). States the transfer obstruction up front: attempts to translate Weil's proofs fail because "in the category of schemes, $\mathrm{spec}\,\mathbb{Z} \times \mathrm{spec}\,\mathbb{Z} = \mathrm{spec}\,\mathbb{Z}$ is one-dimensional" (attributed to Haran 1989, the earliest citation for the obstruction I have seen). Frames the strategy: Bombieri's proof has two steps (Frobenius-flow upper bound; Galois-cover lower bound), and over $\mathbb{Q}$ only the first is needed. On Weil's proofs: "There is some reason to believe that no analogue will ever exist for number fields, or else, that constructing an analogue is harder than establishing the Riemann hypothesis."
- **Section 2 (local/global theory).** Standard Tate-thesis material plus Pellikaan's two-variable zeta function, used to prove the functional equation. Proven, textbook-grade. (Heading depth only this session.)
- **Section 3 (the proof).** A clean self-contained Bombieri proof. The upper bound (Thm 3.6, $N_C(1) \le q + (2g+1)\sqrt q$ for square $q > (g+1)^4$) via the auxiliary function $f(X,Y) = \sum_i a_i(X)\, s_i(Y)$ on $C \times C$, restricted to the Frobenius graph $Y = \phi_q(X)$; injectivity of $f \mapsto f_{|\phi}$ (Lemma 3.4) by pole-order separation; cheap multiplicity by the choice $a_i = b_i^{p^\mu}$, which makes $f_{|\phi}$ a $p^\mu$-th power. The lower bound via Frobenius as symmetries of the Galois closure $C' \to \mathbb{P}^1$, then Lemma 3.1 (Diophantine approximation) converts the two-sided count into $|\omega_\nu| = \sqrt q$. This matches the audit's S1-S7 skeleton move for move.
- **Section 4 ("Comparison with the Riemann Hypothesis").** The proposal. Everything below is from this section unless noted. Everything in it is explicitly speculative; the author's verbs are "could try," "may try," "one could consider," "we do not know," and it closes with "We invite the reader to continue this line of reasoning."

## 2. The model, slot by slot

The load-bearing observation (intro + Remark 3.3 "arith geo"): on $C \times C$ the two coordinates play different roles. For $C = \mathbb{P}^1$, $f(X,Y)$ is a polynomial in $Y$ with coefficients in $X$; this "is analogous to a polynomial in $Y$ with integer coefficients." So $Y$ is the **geometric** coordinate (the variable), $X$ the **arithmetic** coordinate (the coefficients), and Frobenius acts on the geometric coordinate only ($s_i(\phi_q(X)) = s_i^q(X)$). The proposed $\mathbb{Z}$-model instantiates each slot:

| Function-field object | Proposed $\mathbb{Z}$-model object | Status in the paper |
|---|---|---|
| the curve $C$ (geometric coordinate) | the unit disc, coordinate $z \in \mathbb{C}$ | supplied (as a model) |
| the curve $C$ (arithmetic coordinate) | $\mathrm{spec}\,\mathbb{Z}$: integer coefficients $b_i$ | supplied |
| $C \times C$ | functions $f(z) = \sum_i b_i z^{1-i}$, $b_i \in \mathbb{Z}$ | supplied (genuinely two-dimensional data) |
| points over $\mathbb{F}_q$, count $N_C(1)$ | primes as zeros: $z = 1/p$ with multiplicity $[\log_p x]$; a prime "should correspond to $\log p$ points" | supplied via Nevanlinna: $N_f(0,1) = \psi(x)$ for $f(z) = \prod_{p\le x}(1-pz)^{[\log_p x]}$ (exact identity) |
| degree of poles / product formula (S5) | Poisson-Jensen as a sum over all valuations of the field of meromorphic functions; for rational Laurent coefficients the $p$-adic valuations of the leading coefficient close the sum to $0$ | supplied, with a flagged puzzle (the archimedean $v_\infty(c)$ must be excluded) |
| Lemma 3.4 ($a_i$ determined by $f_{|\phi}$) | Taylor coefficients determined by $f$ | supplied ("automatically satisfied") |
| Frobenius flow $\phi_q$ | the shift on $\mathbb{R}$ (per Deninger; per Connes the action of $\mathbb{R}^{>0}$ on $\mathbb{A}/\mathbb{Q}^*$); infinitesimally, **the derivative operator** $d/dz$ | **renamed, not supplied**: no action on the disc is constructed |
| the diagonal $\Delta$ | none | **absent, admitted** |
| cheap multiplicity $a_i = b_i^{p^\mu}$ (S4) | unknown | **absent, admitted** ("We do not know what the analogue of the choice $b_i = a_i^{p^\mu}$ could be") |
| degree bound on the aux function (S6 output) | bound on the Nevanlinna height $T(r,f)$ | **fails downstream**: "we do not know how to bound the Nevanlinna height" |

Two further structural remarks in the section deserve recording. (a) The coordinates-meet-at-one-point reading: the arithmetic and geometric coordinates "meet at $v_0$ (geometrically) and $v_\infty$ (arithmetically). Since this is not a double point, we only see $v_0$." A degenerate, non-transverse crossing in place of a diagonal: PROP-global stated as geometry. (b) The tropical-flavored remark that for large $r$ the archimedean valuations on the circle $\Gamma_r$ are "essentially nonarchimedean" ($|f+g| \approx \max$ off small arcs), conditional on a defect condition. Neither is developed further.

## 3. Where the author himself says it stops (verbatim)

> "The main problem is that the arithmetic coordinate cannot be compared with the geometric coordinate: $z \in \mathbb{C}$ is the geometric coordinate and the coefficients of $f$ are integers, i.e., functions on the arithmetic coordinate $\mathrm{spec}\,\mathbb{Z}$. Hence there is no diagonal and we have to force the vanishing of $f$ at the primes in an artificial manner. As a consequence, we do not know how to bound the Nevanlinna height (i.e., the degree) of this function."

Plus the two admissions already tabled: no analogue of $b_i = a_i^{p^\mu}$, and (footnote) the series $\sum b_i z^{1-i}$ need not converge on the disc, "so we may have to require that it has an analytic continuation." The proposal is honest end to end: nothing is claimed proven beyond the function-field exposition.

## 4. The wall in repo coordinates

**R1 (twice) + PROP-global, exactly as the Stepanov audit predicted; not M4, and not a new coordinate.**

- *No diagonal* $=$ the product base has no self-crossing to intersect the graph with: the PROP-global facet (LEARNINGS #131: the Arakelov face has a carrier but no $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z}) \times \mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z})$ and no $\Gamma_S$). The model genuinely improves on the bare scheme-theoretic degeneration (its function space IS two-dimensional data), but the two coordinates live in different categories ($\mathbb{C}$-analytic vs arithmetic), so the diagonal and the transversality of S1 do not exist. A base without a diagonal is a base without a fixed-point count.
- *No Frobenius action on the geometric coordinate* $=$ R1, operator face. The abstract promises the model "carries an action of Frobenius on the geometric coordinate"; the text delivers only the renaming chain Frobenius flow $\to$ shift on $\mathbb{R}$ (Deninger/Connes) $\to$ derivative operator $d/dz$. Renaming, because nothing plays the role that makes S4 fire: no operation on integer-coefficient series makes vanishing at $\{1/p\}$ cheap. Over $\mathbb{F}_q$ the $p^\mu$-th power buys multiplicity $p^\mu$ at height cost $\approx 1$; over $\mathbb{Z}$ raising to the $p$-th power multiplies the log-height by $p$. Full price. That IS the R1 infinitesimal facet (audit Section 3, S4 row).
- *No positivity is ever consumed.* The section contains no quadratic form, no signature, no Weil-positivity statement; the positivities used (zero multiplicities, $\log(r/|x|) \ge 0$, Jensen) are effectivity-side. Consistent with the audit's M4-in-disguise check coming back negative. (The author's later book has a "Weil positivity" chapter, but that is the function-field theorem, on the Connes side; see Section 6.)

One genuine sharpening the model contributes: the S5 floor becomes an exact integer statement. If $f \in \mathbb{Z}[z]$ vanishes at $1/p$ with multiplicity $m_p$ for every $p \le x$, then $\prod_p (pz-1)^{m_p}$ divides $f$, each factor is primitive, and Gauss's lemma forces the leading coefficient to satisfy $|\mathrm{lead}(f)| \ge \prod_p p^{m_p}$, i.e. log-height $\ge \psi(x)$ for van Frankenhuijsen's multiplicities (or $\ge \theta(x)$ for multiplicity one). So in this model the minimal height of a prime-forced vanisher EQUALS the quantity to be bounded, and vF's own $f$ attains the floor. The audit's Section 3 waste-ratio illustration is, inside the disc model, an exact min-height theorem: the pigeonhole (S3) can never construct below the floor, so the only possible engine is a cheap-multiplicity structure (S4), which is the missing Frobenius. PNT itself is the statement that the floor is $\sim x$.

## 5. The Vojta dictionary tie

The audit found the missing infinitesimal derivation and the missing Frobenius are one object (one structure, $x \mapsto x^{\sqrt q}$, supplies both over $\mathbb{F}_q$). Assessment of van Frankenhuijsen against that: **he makes the identification, openly and in print**: "The infinitesimal generator of the shift on the real line, which is the counterpart of the Frobenius flow by Remark 4.1, is the derivative operator. So we may try to construct such a function that vanishes at the prime numbers to a high order." He neither evades nor hides it; what he does not do is engage Vojta's dictionary: the bibliography has no Vojta entry (Nevanlinna theory is cited via Hayman and Lang-Cherry), so the convergence with the dictionary's famous missing-derivative entry is real but unnamed in the paper.

The dictionary side, verified this session from Vojta's CIME notes (*Diophantine Approximation and Nevanlinna Theory*, Section 29 "Derivatives in Number Theory", read in full from the PDF): "Nevanlinna theory depends in an essential way on the ability to take the derivative of a holomorphic function. In the number field case, on the other hand, there is currently no known counterpart to the derivative." Vojta adds a shape constraint the repo should keep: even in the function-field case the derivative lives in the *absolute* tangent bundle, while the needed counterpart "should live in the *relative* tangent bundle" (relative to the arithmetic base). His Conjecture 29.1 (the "tautological conjecture," the Diophantine analogue of McQuillan's tautological inequality, itself the geometric form of the lemma on the logarithmic derivative) specifies what the missing derivative must DO: to each algebraic point $x$ attach a point $x' \in \mathbb{P}(\Omega_{X/k}(\log D))$ over it with $h_{\mathcal{O}(1)}(x') \le N_S^{(1)}(D,x) + d_k(x) + \varepsilon h_{\mathcal{A}}(x) + O(1)$, with a chain rule (Prop 29.3) making the $x'$ "behave like derivatives," and a successive-minima reading (the $x'$ as the smallest-degree line quotient of $i^*\Omega_{\mathcal{X}/Y}$, Minkowski via Arakelov). It is a named TARGET, not a mechanism, and it is proven exactly where a derivative already exists (curves over char-0 function fields, Remark 29.2). So the two independent 2008 documents localize the same missing object: vF names it Frobenius-as-derivative on the geometric coordinate; Vojta names it the arithmetic derivative in the relative tangent bundle. Same R1 slot, two vocabularies.

## 6. Descendants and state of the art (has anyone supplied the derivation since 2008?)

**No. Absence verified three ways.**

- **Citations of 0806.0044** (Semantic Scholar API, 26 citations, list read in full): the Lapidus fractal-strings school ($p$-adic fractal strings, fractal cohomology 2017), historical surveys (Milne 2015 *The Riemann Hypothesis over Finite Fields*; Oort-Schappacher 2018), Connes-Consani-Marcolli *The Weil Proof and the Geometry of the Adeles Class Space* (SS dates it 2007; likely linked to a related version), genus-4 point-count bounds, quantum-chaos lecture notes, and several crank items. **Zero papers develop the Nevanlinna $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z}) \times \mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z})$ model.**
- **The author's own descendant** (CUP 2014 book, LMS ST 80, subtitle *Frobenius Flow and Shift Operators*; chapters: Valuations / Local theory / Zeta function / Weil positivity / Frobenius flow / Shift operators / Epilogue): per the publisher's chapter abstracts, the weight moved to the Connes shift-operator frame; the Epilogue poses the two-step program (complete Connes' approach for curves, then translate) and notes "the global theory ... is incomplete, both in Chapter 6 of this book, and in the work of Connes and Haran." That is the already-mapped K1 wall (Connes 1998 Theorem 5: global trace-formula positivity $\Leftrightarrow$ RH; see [`Connes-1998-Trace-Formula-NCG-Zeros.md`](Connes-1998-Trace-Formula-NCG-Zeros.md)). So the proposal's own author walked it into the positivity wall rather than supplying the derivation. (Book not read; this is publisher-page depth, flagged.)
- **The Nevanlinna-Diophantine frontier since 2008** (web sweep): progress is exactly on the derivative-FREE entries. Ru-Vojta's general theorem (Amer. J. Math. 142, 2020, birational Nevanlinna constant; extensions to closed subschemes) generalizes Schmidt subspace / SMT *without* the ramification term. McQuillan and Yamanoi proved strong abc *over function fields* (independent proofs, Nevanlinna-side and Ahlfors-side), i.e., where the derivative exists; Yamanoi's algebro-geometric lemma on the logarithmic derivative (Nagoya 2004) and small-function SMT (Acta 2004) are likewise complex/function-field. McQuillan's device in the $1+\varepsilon$ theorem (approximate projection of the derivative to the relative tangent bundle at large height) is the closest thing to a mechanism and is geometric-case only. Difference Nevanlinna theory (Halburd-Korhonen shift analogue of the LLD; from knowledge, search-hit confirmed at survey level) replaces $d/dz$ by $z \mapsto z+c$ entirely inside complex analysis: the same rename-derivative-as-shift move, still no arithmetic side. Mochizuki's claimed abc proof (IUT, PRIMS 2021) is contested (Scholze-Stix) and in any case targets the output inequality, not a derivation mechanism; from knowledge, flagged. **No checkable candidate for the derivation / Frobenius over $\mathbb{Z}$ in this framework exists in the literature as of this sweep.**

## 7. Davenport-Heilbronn discipline note

Architecture-2-style exemption by non-mimicry, and hollow in the same sense as the audit's Section 6. The model's contact with $\zeta$ runs entirely through the Euler side: the disc function is built from the primes ($f = \prod_p (1-pz)^{[\log_p x]}$), the count is $\psi(x)$ (von Mangoldt), and the target inequality $\psi(x) \le x + O(x^{1/2+\varepsilon})$ is Euler-gated. D-H has no Euler product, hence no primes, no $f$, no counting function: the frame cannot be stated for D-H. But since the discriminating structure (the Frobenius action) is exactly the unsupplied slot, the exemption carries no information until the slot is filled; a hypothetical completed model would pass the D-H test by construction. One further note: the paper's Table 4.1 reduction (only the UPPER bound is needed over $\mathbb{Q}$, because $\log|x|$, $x \in \mathbb{Q}$, is dense in $\mathbb{R}$) is the same one-sidedness the audit's S7/Landau row records; it is FE-generic and would hold for D-H too, which is fine because it proves nothing alone.

## 8. Verdict

**Corpus node with the wall author-localized; merges into the existing WATCH slot; one small actionable probe.** Not a live construction program (nothing to continue: the author invited readers in 2008 and none came), not a kill (no obstruction theorem exists; gap, not impossibility). Coordinates gained: the counting road's wall (R1 + PROP-global) now has an independent 2008 publication naming BOTH facets in its own words, plus the Vojta Section 29 convergence giving the missing object a second name and a shape constraint (relative tangent bundle). The audit's one-missing-operator reading is strengthened from the literature side.

**The smallest checkable sub-question** (the Gauss-lemma floor, Section 4): *in the disc model, is the minimal height of an integer-coefficient prime-forced vanisher exactly the Chebyshev function, with zero slack for a pigeonhole?* Concretely: prove/verify that any $f \in \mathbb{Z}[z]$, $f \ne 0$, vanishing at $1/p$ with multiplicity $m_p \ge 1$ for all $p \le x$ satisfies $\log|\mathrm{lead}(f)| \ge \sum_p m_p \log p$, with equality attained by $\prod_p (pz-1)^{m_p}$. A repo probe: (i) Python (sympy), enumerate small $x$, confirm the floor and that no lower-height vanisher exists (integer-exact, K1-clean, RH-independent); (ii) Lean, the divisibility + primitivity + Gauss's-lemma chain (Mathlib has `Polynomial.content` and Gauss's lemma), as a companion to `IsogenyDegree`/`ArakelovAssembly`: a machine-checked statement that vF's model has NO Siegel-lemma route, so any future completion must supply the S4 cheap-multiplicity structure, i.e., the R1 operator. Frames the negative as a coordinate: the model's open slot is provably the only slot.
