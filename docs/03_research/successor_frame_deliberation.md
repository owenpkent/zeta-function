## 1. The decision, in one paragraph

The successor frame is **the funding-boundary frame**: a frontier ATTACK frame on the
wall's limit clause at the compressed-Weil-form register that Alpöge-Furman (2608.13637)

residual set sits. The frame's central target is to convert that information wall into a
THEOREM-SHAPE about the certificate class (or find the certificate family that evades it,
which would be frontier motion), making one piece of the four-level compass a theorem at
the record-holding register. The recovery register (#205) enters as the frame's
counting-side pricing instrument when and only when a frame question needs it, never as
the metric. Of the audit's four doors: #188 horizon-optimality is consumed (it was

## 4. The frame: two walls at one register, and the convergence question

**The register.** The AF compression $\tilde G$: a $d \approx N(T,2T)$-dimensional
compression of Weil's Hermitian form; the prime side funds $\mathrm{tr}$ and
$\|\cdot\|_{HS}^2$ unconditionally at Fourier support 1; each off-line pair pays exactly
one unit of positive index (FE pairing + Sylvester inertia); a rank-trace inequality
converts the budget into $N_0^s \ge (2 - R(\psi) - o(1))N$.

**Wall 1, the funding wall (F1's target: TYPE it).** The unconditional funding ends at
support 1 because that is where the proven mean values end; beyond, the off-diagonal
budget consumes prime-pair correlation data at INTEGER shifts (the Hardy-Littlewood
class: counts of $\{p - q = h\}$ with singular-series weights; $\{\log p - \log q\}$ is
only the Fourier-side parametrization), and in-paper, unconditionally "higher moments add
nothing" (the Rudnick-Sarnak diagonal range). Two ledger objects must be kept DISTINCT
here (adversary A2): **L1**, #152's additive lattice (integer counting $= x + O(1)$,
Poisson, the theta FE: the clause the HL singular series lives on, congruence data of the
primes inside $\mathbb{Z}$; in #152's own wording, $\mathbb{Q}$-linear independence of
$\{\log p\}$ is the EULER-side half, not this clause); and **L2**, log-space
commensurability of $\{\log p\}$ (what #162 localized and #188/#205 priced in the digits
currency). The repo's own controls anti-align them: SNAP is fully L2-commensurate while
destroying the HL class; TRUE is L2-independent while carrying full L1. So F1's claim is
SPLIT. **F1a (posability): the HL data class exists as a data class only over an additive
lattice (L1), so the funding wall consumes the conservation law's lattice clause.** Test:
the Beurling PRIME side, which IS posable (#202(iv) kills only the zero/FE side) and is
exactly the density-alone comparator (a generic Beurling system's off-diagonals should
evaluate from density squared, with no singular series), plus the e2bc ramp axis and
synthetic lattices. Register stated in advance: the singular series has average 1, so the
density-alone model matches at LEADING order and the lattice enters at the second-order
term (the Montgomery-Soundararajan register); F1a's typing lives there, not at leading
order. Pre-registered competitor typing: the obstruction to funding past support 1 is
minor-arc cancellation / short-interval variance (an architecture-4 species), not
lattice-data availability; F1 must discriminate against this competitor or grade itself
inconclusive. **F1b (pricing): whether support extension is priced in the #205 digits
currency is OPEN, not inherited**: #205 priced the L2 membership class; whether the
funding wall's data class reduces to anything L2-priced is exactly F1's question, not an
input. If F1a lands, the wall statement gains its counting-side quantitative register; if
it dies (off-diagonals purchasable from density alone at the stated register, or the
competitor typing wins), that is recorded and the frame re-scopes to Wall 2 alone. Named
negative control (#204(v)): the Vedana classification shows formula-existence is
system-generic and its uniqueness clause consumes no additive lattice, so any typing
argument that would run identically on a generic Vedana-class system is wrong.

**Wall 2, the information wall (F2's target: ATTACK it).** In-paper: full HL* yields 100
percent SIMPLE, which is still not RH; "RH itself is out of reach of the mechanism"; the
bandwidth-one certificate class is priced at $\approx 0.682$. The paper's ceiling covers
rank/index arguments at bounded Fourier support. The class-level question it leaves open,
which is the frame's central target: **can ANY sequence of finite-rank certificates whose
inputs are prime-correlation data (any support, granted freely) certify
location-completeness in the limit; and if not, is the thing the limit certificate needs
beyond correlations exactly the uniformity/determinant-class clause (#148), i.e. M4 seen
from the counting side?** The expected answer per the compass is no-and-yes, and making
that a theorem-shape would be the first PROVEN instance of the Level 3/Level 4 boundary
at a live, record-holding register (CLAUDE.md's own caveat: that Level 4 equals
positivity is "a finding so far, not a theorem"; this is the named chance to upgrade a
piece of it). The evasion branch is equally real: a certificate family that consumes
correlations at growing support and pins location completely in the limit would
contradict the compass and move the frontier; the branch structure is what makes this an
attack with failure states rather than a measurement.

**Complementarity bookkeeping** (so the frame starts from the map, not from zero): #199
certifies the line meter cannot see WHERE off-line zeros are; #202 shows the prime side
bounds HOW MANY there can be; the gap between HOW MANY and ALL is exactly what Wall 2
formalizes. The mechanism datum ("indefiniteness is countable at finite rank from prime
data; positivity is not certifiable there") is the frame's seed statement: F2 asks
whether its second half is a theorem about the whole certificate class.

**What the frame does NOT do**: chase the in-class $\approx 0.682$ constant (ruled out at
the AF note's Section 4: the wall is the support, not the point); re-run bank probes (the

The dossier's phrases "HL-type additive correlations of $\{\log p\}$" (Section 1) and "HL-type structure on $\{\log p - \log q\}$" (Section 4) locate the data in log-space, where it does not live.

**(ii) The conflation is real, and it is checkable on the repo's own controls.** Two different objects are spliced by the phrase "additive lattice":

- L1: #152's additive lattice, quoted at source: "the separator is the ADDITIVE lattice (N = x + O(1) / Poisson / theta FE)", and the fourth clause: "the glue must be LATTICE-CONSUMING: it must use the fact that the SAME set is multiplicatively free (Euler, the circles) and additively a perfect lattice (Poisson, the FE)."
