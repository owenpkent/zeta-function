# Attempted new math: the Euler-Sen polarization package

> Written 2026-06-04 in response to "try to invent the new math needed." This is
> a proposal, not a result. It is designed to attack the exact gap isolated by
> Direction 8A: the missing Hodge-Riemann/Rosati positivity on the arithmetic
> `H^1`, while respecting the soft-detector wall and the WCart finite-model
> failure.

## One-line idea

Petrov non-semisimplicity should not be treated as a defect to diagonalize away.
It should be treated as **monodromy**. The missing positivity should be a
mixed-Hodge-style primitive polarization

```text
Q_k(x,y) = Tr(x cup N_E^k C_E y)
```

where `N_E` is the nilpotent Euler-Sen monodromy and `C_E` is a Weil operator
determined by the Euler/Frobenius direction, not by the Sen weight grading alone.

This is the structural move that 2KK did not test. 2KK used a diagonal Hodge-star
phase on the rank-2 cup support and got a rigid hyperbolic form. In classical
Hodge theory, the cure for a non-semisimple degeneration is not a diagonal star;
it is a monodromy weight filtration plus primitive forms involving powers of the
nilpotent operator.

## Why this is the right shape

The WCart probe failed for a clean reason: the exact derivation-compatible cup
form with `Theta` semisimple is supported only on the weight pair `(0,1)`, so any
diagonal Hodge-star produces signature `(1,1)` on that rank-2 block. That route
has the wrong polarity.

Petrov says the real Sen operator is not semisimple. Instead of asking for an
eigenspace polarization, define the arithmetic analogue of a limiting mixed
Hodge structure:

```text
(H, W_E, F_arch, N_E, cup, Tr)
```

with:

- `H`: the global arithmetic `H^1`;
- `W_E`: the Euler weight filtration generated by the local `(1,p)` Frobenius
  bidegrees and the archimedean comparison;
- `F_arch`: the archimedean/Hodge filtration, carried by the de Rham point or
  the archimedean Green metric;
- `N_E`: the nilpotent part of the Euler-Sen operator, measuring failure of the
  conjugate/Nygaard filtration to split globally;
- `cup : H x H -> H^2`;
- `Tr : H^2 -> C(-1)`, the Euler-pole fundamental class.

The decisive change is that the primitive positivity is not

```text
Tr(x cup star y)
```

with `star` diagonal in Sen weights. It is

```text
Q_k(x,y) = (-1)^{k(k-1)/2} Tr(x cup N_E^k C_E y)
```

on the primitive part

```text
P_k = ker(N_E^{k+1} : Gr^W_k H -> Gr^W_{-k-2} H).
```

This is the same formal pattern as the Hodge-Riemann bilinear relations for
degenerations: the nilpotent operator supplies the missing primitive direction.

## What is new here

The proposed object is an **Euler-Sen mixed Hodge structure**:

```text
EulerSenMHS(H) :=
  W_E      -- Euler/Frobenius weight filtration
  F_arch   -- archimedean Hodge filtration
  N_E      -- nilpotent Sen monodromy
  cup      -- FE/Poincare duality
  Tr       -- Euler-pole target
```

subject to four axioms.

**Axiom 1: Euler formation.** `W_E`, `N_E`, and `Tr` can be formed only from
Euler-product/Frobenius data. No Euler product means no `C(-1)` target and no
Euler-Sen package. This is the formation guard now encoded in Lean by
`FrobeniusAlgebra.lean`.

**Axiom 2: monodromy weight relation.** `N_E W_m subset W_{m-2}`, and for every
`r >= 0`,

```text
N_E^r : Gr^W_r H ~= Gr^W_{-r} H
```

after the Tate twist dictated by the local `(1,p)` bidegrees.

**Axiom 3: Euler Weil operator.** `C_E` is not the diagonal Sen-weight phase. It
is the involution/complex structure induced by the Frobenius half of the
Euler-Sen package. It must satisfy:

```text
C_E^2 = (-1)^weight
Omega(C_E x, C_E y) = Omega(x,y)
```

where `Omega(x,y) = Tr(x cup y)`, and it must specialize over `F_q` to the usual
Weil operator giving Rosati positivity.

**Axiom 4: primitive positivity.** On every primitive piece `P_k`,

```text
Q_k(x,x) > 0  for x != 0
```

with the standard Hodge-Riemann signs.

The conjecture is: the global prismatization/WCart arithmetic `H^1` of
`Spec(Z)` carries an Euler-Sen mixed Hodge structure.

## Why this might evade the known traps

**D-H discipline.** Davenport-Heilbronn has no Euler product, so Axiom 1 blocks
formation. This is structural K2, not a detector.

**2KK wrong polarity.** The 2KK hyperbolic `(1,1)` result applies to a diagonal
Hodge-star on the rank-2 semisimple cup support. The Euler-Sen package uses
primitive forms with `N_E^k`; the rank-2 hyperbolic block is no longer the whole
polarization.

**Petrov obstruction.** Petrov non-semisimplicity becomes evidence for the right
formalism. If the Sen operator were semisimple, the package would collapse back
to the failed diagonal-star route.

**de Branges too-strong failure.** The de Branges pairing globalizes the
continuation but imposes positivity pointwise/too strongly. The Euler-Sen
proposal is not pointwise in zeros; it is primitive-piece positivity from
Euler/Frobenius monodromy, closer to standard conjectures.

**Marginal positivity.** The zero slack over `Z` is compatible with a primitive
polarization theorem: Hodge-Riemann signs are exact, not buffered numerical
inequalities.

## First falsifiable theorem to prove

Before touching prismatic geometry, prove the following pure linear-algebra
theorem.

> **Finite Euler-Sen HR lemma.** Let `H` be a finite-dimensional complex vector
> space with a nondegenerate alternating form `Omega`, an increasing filtration
> `W`, a nilpotent `N` with `N W_m subset W_{m-2}` and
> `N^r : Gr^W_r H ~= Gr^W_{-r} H`, and an operator `C_E` satisfying the Weil
> compatibility axioms above. Then the primitive forms
> `Q_k(x,y) = (-1)^{k(k-1)/2} Omega(x, N^k C_E y)` have Hodge-Riemann signature
> if and only if `C_E` is a positive compatible complex structure on each
> primitive quotient.

This theorem is not RH. It is the missing local formalism: it says exactly what
kind of extra structure must be built on WCart to avoid 2KK's hyperbolic failure.
It is Lean-sized linear algebra once the filtration and graded pieces are
modeled.

**First finite probe landed (2LL, 2026-06-04).** The toy theorem survives the
cheap kill in the finite function-field specialization:
[`../../../experiments/arithmetic_geometric/e2ll_euler_sen_polarization.md`](../../../experiments/arithmetic_geometric/e2ll_euler_sen_polarization.md).
Putting a genuine defective block at the Tate center,
`Theta = -1/2 I + N`, gives an exact cup equation
`Theta^T Omega + Omega Theta = -Omega`; the primitive monodromy form
`Omega(x, N y)` recovers the Euler/Rosati matrix `B_E`; and for
`B_E = [[2g,t],[t,2gq]]` positivity is exactly `t^2 < 4g^2q`. The 2KK diagonal-star
baseline remains rigidly hyperbolic. Honest caveat: `B_E` is supplied as input,
so the real arithmetic construction of `B_E` is still the gap.

## First mathematical construction target

Build `C_E` from the Euler/Frobenius direction by a polar decomposition, not from
Sen weights:

```text
A_E := Omega^{-1} B_E
C_E := A_E (-A_E^2)^{-1/2}
```

where `B_E` is the Frobenius/Rosati trace form assembled from the local `(1,p)`
bidegrees plus the archimedean Green metric. Over a finite field this recovers
the usual Rosati complex structure. Over `Z`, this is the exact place where the
new theorem is needed: prove `-A_E^2` has a positive functional calculus on the
primitive Euler-Sen quotients without using RH.

This is dangerous because `B_E` resembles the Weil form. The non-circular version
must define `B_E` as a geometric trace on the Euler-Sen object, not as a
zero-side or positivity-assumed kernel.

## Kill conditions

Abandon or demote this proposal if any of these happens:

1. The finite Euler-Sen HR lemma collapses to ordinary positivity of `B_E` with
   no independent formation content. That would be K1 circularity.
2. `C_E` can be formed from functional-equation/archimedean data alone. Then it
   is D-H-blind and dead.
3. In a faithful WCart finite model with a genuine defective Sen block, the
   monodromy primitive form still has rigid `(1,1)` polarity independent of
   Euler data.
4. The finite-field specialization fails to recover Rosati positivity on
   `C x C`.
