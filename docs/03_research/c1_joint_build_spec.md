## 2. The chosen candidate, and why this one

Direction 10B ([`research_directions/10B_thh_weight_and_mobius.md`](research_directions/10B_thh_weight_and_mobius.md)) is the only place in the repo that already poses concrete, named, unresolved sub-questions about *this exact object* (the ghost/THH self-product, TR $\cong$ W per #150): **Gap A**, does the scaling operator $N$ built from $F_nV_n = n$ act on the degree-$2i$ graded piece of $\mathrm{TP}(\mathbb{Z})$ with eigenvalue $i$, and **Gap B**, does the $\mathrm{THH}\to\mathrm{TC}$ cyclotomic equalizer ($\mathrm{TC} = \mathrm{eq}(\mathrm{can}, \varphi)$, Nikolaus-Scholze) realize the necklace/Möbius inversion that turns the level-counting series $-\zeta'$ into the primitive Euler-product series $-\zeta'/\zeta$. Both gaps have sat unrevisited since 2026-05-30; the low-level necklace check (Gap B's cheapest piece) is done ([`e_necklace_mobius.py`](../../experiments/homotopy/e_necklace_mobius.py)), but the load-bearing steps (the eigenvalue claim, the equalizer's actual multiplicativity) were never executed.

**The two-sentence justification.** Gap A and Gap B are picked over a fresh construction (for instance, building a Nikolaus-Scholze fracture square from scratch, or re-entering the just-closed CCM corridor in algebraic clothing) because they are the only previously named, citation-grounded, unresolved sub-questions already on record for the ghost/THH self-product, so testing them computationally is a build at a joint rather than another survey. Both already carry a precise, pre-existing risk flag (Gap A's eigenvalue claim was never pinned; Gap B was flagged by an adversary in the same note as "a likely category error," the equalizer being an additive limit against a multiplicative target), so a computational test of either lands on a genuine SP3c advance or a cleanly nameable wall, the same pre-registered-falsifier discipline that just closed the CCM corridor cleanly (#163/#164).

## (a) The object

**Carrier.** The finite ghost/THH self-product, built by gluing two pieces that already exist separately in the repo and are known to be "one mechanism, two costumes" (#150, TR $\cong$ W): (i) the Witt/Borger ghost lattice on $\{1, \dots, N\}$ with Frobenius $F_k$ and Verschiebung $V_k$, $k = 2..12$ ([`e2ai_base_battery.py`](../../experiments/arithmetic_geometric/e2ai_base_battery.py), base B3), plus the Bökstedt torsion data $\log|\mathrm{THH}_{2i-1}(\mathbb{Z})| = \log i$ (base B4); and (ii) the necklace-polynomial combinatorics of the cyclotomic $C_n$-fixed points, $M(q,n) = \tfrac1n \sum_{d\mid n}\mu(n/d)q^d$ ([`e_necklace_mobius.py`](../../experiments/homotopy/e_necklace_mobius.py)). These have never been glued into one module; B3/B4 and the necklace check are separate probes today.

**Operator/map (two, neither previously built).**

1. $N_{\mathrm{op}}$, the **Gap A scaling operator**: the candidate generator of the $\mathbb{N}^\times$-action on the $\sigma$-graded $\mathrm{TP}(\mathbb{Z})$, assembled from $F_nV_n = n$, tested at finite truncation for whether its eigenvalue on the level-$i$ graded piece is genuinely $i$, and (the new question this spec adds) whether *any* operator built purely from $\{F_k, V_k\}$ has spectral data that depends on more than the abstract index $k$.
2. A finite truncated model of the **Gap B TC equalizer**, $\mathrm{TC}(\mathbb{Z}) = \mathrm{eq}(\mathrm{can}, \varphi : \mathrm{TC}^-(\mathbb{Z}) \to \mathrm{TP}(\mathbb{Z}))$: a candidate arithmetic function $f_{\mathrm{TC}}(n)$ built from the necklace weights $M(q,n)$ composed with the Bökstedt-torsion data at level $n$, meant to model the equalizer's Euler characteristic, tested for whether it is *multiplicative* ($f(mn) = f(m)f(n)$ for $\gcd(m,n)=1$, the Euler-product signature W6 needs) or only *additive* (direct-sum-shaped, the adversary's flagged failure mode).

**Data consumed.** Only the multiplicative/divisor-lattice structure of the index set $\{1, \dots, N\}$, reused unchanged from B3/B4's own inputs (Bökstedt's cited theorem, the necklace identity), plus, for the discipline sweep, the generalized-integer structure from [`beurling.py`](../../experiments/_shared/beurling.py)'s `BeurlingSystem`. No zeta zero data enters any construction step (Section (c)).

## (b) The tests

Three phases, each a genuine falsifier (an outcome that would overturn or narrow a load-bearing claim), not a measurement pre-destined to grade UNMOVED. This design choice directly answers the frame audit's own Choice G lesson (#163): "'Frontier: UNMOVED' as the grade is a bookkeeping CHOICE... K1-clean finite probes on this family are then structurally guaranteed to grade UNMOVED." Each phase below is built so that BOTH outcomes are informative.

**Phase 0. The anchor: translate, don't just re-verify.** LEARNINGS #151's headline finding, per-prime W6 exists exactly, currently lives entirely in analytic language (Poisson summation on $\mathbb{R}/(\log p)\mathbb{Z}$, [`e2aj_w6_gluing.py`](../../experiments/arithmetic_geometric/e2aj_w6_gluing.py) checks 1-2). Before Phase 1/2 mean anything, BUILDER must exhibit that identity as a statement about the **p-typical sub-lattice** of the B3/B4 object itself: restrict the ghost lattice to the powers $\{1, p, p^2, \dots\}$ of one fixed prime (the standard p-typical decomposition of the big Witt vectors; flag for SURVEYOR confirmation, this is cited not re-derived here), and show that the resulting $F_p/V_p$ data reproduces, via an explicit map BUILDER must construct, e2aj's own two numbers: the Poisson identity $\log p \sum_k g(k\log p) = \sum_n \hat g(2\pi n/\log p)$ to max defect $< 10^{-10}$ (matching e2aj's reported bound over $p \in \{2,3,5\}$, $s \in \{0.7, 1.3\}$), and the Euler-factor pole location $1 - p^{-s} = 0$ at $s = 2\pi i n/\log p$ to $< 10^{-12}$. **Pass**: the map exists and reproduces both numbers to those tolerances. **Fail**: no such map can be built from $F_p/V_p$ alone, in which case "ghost/THH self-product" is not yet an honest label for #151's finding, and this is recorded as an earlier, narrower wall than Phase 1 (Section (d)).

**Phase 1. Gap A operationalized, Beurling-twinned (the size-sensitivity test).** Build $N_{\mathrm{op}}$ on the B3 ghost lattice ($N = 720$, matching e2ai's own truncation) and every other operator obtainable from $\{F_k, V_k : k = 2..12\}$ by composition (in particular the commutators $[F_a, V_b]$, $a \ne b$). Compute every existing B3/B4/necklace invariant (the $F_kV_k = k\,\mathrm{Id}$ relation, $\mathrm{tr}(V_kF_k)$, $\mathrm{tr}(F_k) = 0$, the Bökstedt log-torsion identity, $M(q,n)$ for $q,n \le 12$) exactly (integer/exact-rational arithmetic, no floating tolerance, matching e2ai's own "EXACT ARITHMETIC" convention). Then rebuild the *entire* battery with the index set relabeled by a `BeurlingSystem` generator list (`b_p = p\exp(\varepsilon_p)`, the existing default fake) in place of the rational primes, using `BeurlingSystem.gen_integers(x, with_factorization=True)` for the exponent-vector data exactly as [`e2ak`](../../experiments/arithmetic_geometric/e2ak_beurling_discipline.py)'s C4 check already does for B3's $\Lambda$-recovery alone. **Pass (a positive discovery)**: at least one invariant, especially any eigenvalue of $N_{\mathrm{op}}$, changes value under the Beurling relabeling, i.e. depends on generator *size* ($\log b_p$) and not merely on index rank. **Wall (the predicted outcome)**: nothing changes; every checked invariant is Beurling-identical, extending #152's C4 finding (which showed this only for B3's $\Lambda$-recovery mechanism) to the full B3/B4/necklace/Gap-A apparatus.

**Phase 2. Gap B operationalized (the multiplicativity test).** Construct $f_{\mathrm{TC}}(n)$ for $n \le 500$ (matching B4's own `NMAX`) from the necklace weights and the Bökstedt-torsion data, using *only* citation-grounded inputs (never fit against $\Lambda(n)$ or any zeta coefficient; see K1 in Section (c)). Test $f_{\mathrm{TC}}(mn) = f_{\mathrm{TC}}(m)f_{\mathrm{TC}}(n)$ exactly (`fractions.Fraction`, matching `e_necklace_mobius.py`'s own convention) over every coprime pair $(m,n)$ with $mn \le 500$. **Pass**: the identity holds on that whole grid, upgrading Gap B from "flagged category error" to "measured multiplicative," a genuine SP3c advance. **Wall (the predicted outcome, per the standing adversary flag)**: it fails on a generic coprime pair, confirming computationally that the equalizer construction is additive-shaped, not multiplicative, the same overcounting failure mode e2aj already measured for the naive direct-sum gluing (45x at $T=100$), now diagnosed as a category property rather than a scale problem.

## (c) The disciplines

**Beurling.** The discipline is not satisfied by a check literally erroring; it is satisfied by naming which clause a construction that passes identically for the fake fails to pay. Phase 1 is built exactly to produce that name: if (as predicted) every invariant is Beurling-identical, the nameable failing clause is that the ghost/THH combinatorial layer consumes only the abstract divisor-lattice/monoid structure of the index set and never a generator's actual size, so it cannot pay the fourth clause instituted at rung 3 (LATTICE-CONSUMING, #152). This is a strict extension, not a repeat, of #152's own C4 result, which tested only B3's $\Lambda$-recovery mechanism; Phase 1 sweeps the trace identities, the Bökstedt log-torsion identity, the necklace polynomials, and the never-before-built Gap A operator through the same twin.

**Davenport-Heilbronn.** Structurally exempt, on the same grounds already established for this whole probe family (e2ai, e2aj): every object here (the ghost lattice, the necklace polynomials, $\mathrm{THH}(\mathbb{Z})$) is built *from* $\mathbb{Z}$'s unique-factorization monoid, the Euler-product carrier. D-H has no Euler product and hence no monoid, so the construction is unstateable for it (AX-FORM), exactly the Architecture-2 exemption CLAUDE.md already carves out ("Deninger-style constructions intentionally require the Euler product that D-H lacks"). No D-H check belongs in this rung's battery; noting the exemption explicitly is the discipline.

**K1 guard.** No zeta zero location (no `mp.zetazero`, no fitted $\gamma_n$) enters any construction step in Phase 0, 1, or 2. The only permitted appearance of zero data is as an external sanity control, reusing e2aj's `ZETA_ZEROS` landmark list purely to sanity-check the Riemann-von Mangoldt main term if Phase 0's translation needs one, never as an ingredient of $N_{\mathrm{op}}$ or $f_{\mathrm{TC}}$. The specific K1 risk unique to this rung: $f_{\mathrm{TC}}(n)$ in Phase 2 must be built *only* from the necklace weights and the cited Bökstedt torsion, never calibrated or curve-fit against $\Lambda(n)$ or any known zeta coefficient to make the multiplicativity test pass; doing so would install the answer rather than compute it, the exact failure mode e1l/e1k already named ("installed... NOT a W6 Betti count computed by symmetry").

## (d) The grading

Three tiers, read directly off the e1l/e1p precedent rather than invented fresh:

1. **W6-genuine (computed by symmetry).** The identity is forced by a manifestly satisfied structural hypothesis (an actual cited theorem's hypotheses hold, not a numerical coincidence at tested truncations), is family-uniform (independent of $N$, of the window, of generator labeling), and survives the Beurling twin for a reason it can name (or is exempt because it is a cited per-prime theorem, as Phase 0's target already is). This is the e1l bar: "a TOPOLOGICAL invariant the theory computes by its own symmetry," not "installed BY the window."
2. **Measured / installed.** The identity holds numerically at tested truncations but depends on a construction choice not manifestly forced by the cited theorems (a specific $N$, a specific grading, a specific weighting in $f_{\mathrm{TC}}$), or it passes identically for the Beurling twin (Beurling-blind, hence generic rather than W6-genuine), the e1p bar: "HOLDS as a MEASURED PROFILE... NOT graded a theorem instance, because... the textbook hypothesis is not manifestly met."
3. **Blind / category error / fail.** The construction produces no discriminating identity at all (Phase 1's predicted outcome), or is shown to be a structural category mismatch (Phase 2's predicted outcome, additive vs. multiplicative), or fails the Phase 0 anchor outright.

**The pre-registered wall statement.** The single most likely result, and the one this spec commits to in advance: Phase 1 finds that the ghost/THH combinatorial layer (Witt $F_k/V_k$, necklace/Möbius, and any operator assembled from them including $N_{\mathrm{op}}$) is index-only and label-invariant, hence Beurling-insensitive by construction, regardless of how Phase 0 or Phase 2 resolve. If this is what happens, it should be recorded as the next LEARNINGS entry (continuing the #150-#153/#160/#163-#165 numbering), stated as: Gap A closes (not "proves," but "is shown structurally incapable of supplying the needed channel"), Direction 10B is updated to mark Gap A resolved-negatively rather than open, and C1's derived-base half is narrowed to a single remaining question, whether a genuinely metric/archimedean ingredient can be grafted onto the combinatorial skeleton without collapsing back into the just-closed CCM corridor's own territory. That narrowing is itself the kind of progress this project's mindset asks for (a coordinate ruled out, not a dead end): it would mean C1's two live sub-fronts, the CCM analytic route and the derived-base algebraic route, converge on needing the *same* ingredient (an explicit size/metric channel), which is new information neither route supplies alone today.
