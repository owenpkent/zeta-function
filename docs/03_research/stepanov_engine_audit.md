## 1. Why this audit

Over $\mathbb{F}_q$ the RH for curves has at least two proofs that never touch a polarization. Weil's proof runs on the Hodge index theorem (form-shaped). Stepanov (1969) and Bombieri (1974) proved the same theorem with auxiliary functions and degree counts: no surface intersection form, no signature, no positivity of a quadratic form anywhere in the argument. (Deligne's Weil I is a third shape, tensor-power amplification, whose positivity input is coefficient non-negativity of an Euler product, the passivity side in the acoustic frame, not a signature; noted here for completeness, known at textbook depth only.)

The project has exhaustively mapped where form-shaped roads wall over $\mathbb{Z}$: M4, the arithmetic Hodge standard conjecture (LEARNINGS #128, #131, #132). It has never asked where the counting-shaped road walls. The working prediction under test: it walls at Frobenius-existence (the R1 facet of [`sourcing_gap_r1.md`](sourcing_gap_r1.md)), not at M4. Verdict in Section 6: **confirmed, with one refinement**.

## 2. The engine skeleton: seven named moves

Sources: Stepanov 1969 (hyperelliptic curves, prime field); Bombieri, Seminaire Bourbaki expose 430 (1972/73), the general-curve version; Schmidt, LNM 536 (1976), the textbook treatment; Tao's 2014 exposition (read in full this session) and Kowalski's 2010 note on Bombieri's version (read in full this session). Reading-depth flags in Section 8.

- **S1 (FROBENIUS-GRAPH).** $\#C(\mathbb{F}_q)$ is the intersection of two curves inside $C \times C$: the diagonal $\Delta = \{(z,z)\}$ and the graph $\Gamma_\phi = \{(z, \phi z)\}$ of the $q$-power (in Bombieri-Tao, the $\sqrt q$-power) Frobenius. The count to be bounded is a fixed-point count of an explicit endomorphism of controlled degree $q$.
- **S2 (AUX-SPACE).** Fix $P_\infty \in C$; let $R_n = L(nP_\infty)$ be the rational functions with poles only at $P_\infty$ of order $\le n$. The auxiliary space is $V = R_\ell \otimes R_m$, functions on $C \times C$ with controlled pole divisor.
- **S3 (RIEMANN-COUNT).** Riemann's inequality (the weak half of Riemann-Roch): $\dim R_n \ge n + 1 - g$, hence $\dim V \ge (\ell+1-g)(m+1-g)$. Pure dimension counting; no duality, no positivity of a form.
- **S4 (FROBENIUS-CHEAP MULTIPLICITY).** The characteristic-$p$ miracle. Restricted to the graph, elements of $V$ become $\sum_i f_i \cdot g_i^{\sqrt q}$ (Frobenius pullback is a $\sqrt q$-th power), and $\sqrt q$-th powers are differentially flat: all Hasse derivatives up to order $\sqrt q - 1$ vanish for free. So linear algebra (rank-nullity; the pigeonhole that over $\mathbb{Z}$ would be a Siegel lemma) produces a nonzero auxiliary function vanishing at every $\mathbb{F}_q$-point with high effective multiplicity, at a per-point cost far below the multiplicity purchased. Stepanov's original form imposes Hasse-derivative vanishing directly; Bombieri's form buys the multiplicity by taking high powers. Same mechanism: Frobenius makes vanishing cheap.
- **S5 (DEGREE-BOUND).** On a projective curve, a nonzero function has $\deg(\text{zeros}) = \deg(\text{poles})$ (degree of a principal divisor is zero; the function-field product formula). So $(\text{multiplicity}) \times N \le \ell + m\sqrt q$.
- **S6 (OPTIMIZE).** Choose $\ell, m$ (Bombieri: $q$ a perfect square, $q > (g+1)^4$) to get the one-sided bound $N \le q + (2g+1)\sqrt q + 1$.
- **S7 (TWO-SIDED-VIA-FE).** Convert one-sided to two-sided. Tao's exposition uses a Galois/reducible-curve twist trick; Bombieri's assembly runs the upper bound over every extension $\mathbb{F}_{q^n}$ and feeds it into the rationality and functional equation of $Z(C,T)$ (F.K. Schmidt 1931, itself elementary via Riemann-Roch): upper bounds at every level pin $|\alpha_i| \le \sqrt q$ for the finitely many inverse roots, and the FE $\alpha \mapsto q/\alpha$ upgrades $\le$ to $=$. That equality is $|N - (q+1)| \le 2g\sqrt q$.

The RH-producing content is concentrated in S1 + S4: Frobenius as an explicit endomorphism of degree $q$ whose graph is transverse to the diagonal, and Frobenius as the source of cheap multiplicity. Everything else is bookkeeping (S2, S3, S5, S6) or classical analytic conversion (S7). **No move consumes a signature.** Over $\mathbb{F}_q$ the premise of this audit checks out: a genuinely non-polarization proof of the function-field RH exists.

## 3. The transfer table: each move over $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z})$

| Move | What it needs | Status over $\mathbb{Z}$ | Gap facet |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 Frobenius-graph | a non-identity endomorphism of bounded degree with graph transverse to $\Delta$, fixed points = the objects counted | **ABSENT.** The only ring endomorphism of $\mathbb{Z}$ is the identity, and by Fermat's little theorem the identity IS a Frobenius lift at every $p$ ($n^p \equiv n \bmod p$): $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z})$ is canonically a $\Lambda$-ring with trivial Frobenii (Borger's $\mathbb{F}_1$-descent). So $\Gamma_\phi = \Delta$, the intersection degenerates, the fixed-point set is everything, and the count has no content. | S3 Riemann-count | a dimension lower bound = pigeonhole | **PRESENT.** The Siegel lemma (Bombieri-Vaaler 1983) is the arithmetic Riemann inequality; van der Geer-Schoof (2000) give the exact arithmetic Riemann-Roch $h^0 - h^1 = \deg$ with Poisson summation as Serre duality | none |
| S4 cheap multiplicity | a derivation to define high-order vanishing + a structure making it cheap | **ABSENT in the prime direction.** No derivation $\mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}$; the known substitutes are Buium's $p$-derivations $\delta_p(n) = (n - n^p)/p$ (Fermat quotients, one prime at a time, $p$-adic, no global assembly). | S5 degree-bound | degree of a principal divisor is zero | **PRESENT.** The product formula $\sum_v \log|a|_v = 0$; Liouville's inequality ("a nonzero integer has $|n| \ge 1$") is the working form in every transcendence proof. | S7 two-sided-via-FE | rationality + FE + a one-sided-to-two-sided conversion | **PRESENT in analytic form.** $\xi(s) = \xi(1-s)$ is a theorem; Landau's oscillation theorem ($\psi(x) - x = \Omega_\pm(x^{\Theta - \epsilon})$, non-negative-coefficient Dirichlet series) means a one-sided Stepanov-type bound $\psi(x) \le x + O(x^{1/2+\epsilon})$ would already force RH. Rationality (finitely many zeros) fails, but the conversion machinery does not need it in this form | none (the FE is consequence-level, per 2A Step C, but only the consequence is consumed) |

A degenerate computation makes S1's failure vivid. Over $\mathbb{F}_q$ the universal vanisher at all rational points of $\mathbb{A}^1$ is $T^q - T$: degree $q$, point count $q$, waste ratio exactly $1$. This zero-waste identity is what seeds the whole method. Over $\mathbb{Z}$ the universal vanisher at the primes $p \le x$ is $\prod_{p \le x} p$, of log-size $\theta(x)$ against count $\pi(x)$: the waste ratio $\theta(x)/\pi(x) \sim \log x$ diverges, and the statement that the vanisher's size is $\sim x$ IS the prime number theorem. The engine's basic bookkeeping identity over $\mathbb{Z}$ is the prime-counting problem itself, not a tool for it. (Illustration, not a theorem: it shows the fixed-point structure degenerating, in exactly the #42/#128 sense that every unconditional handle lives where the Euler product converges.)

## 4. The wall, named precisely

**The wall is R1 (Frobenius-existence / sourcing), with a PROP-global rider (the product base), and it is not M4 in disguise.**

The honesty check the task demanded: could the wall be M4 wearing counting clothes? Every place a positivity could hide was checked:

1. **S3 (dimension count).** Riemann's inequality is a lower bound on $h^0$, proved by linear algebra over the function field; its $\mathbb{Z}$-analogue (Siegel lemma / geometry of numbers) is Minkowski-type convex-body counting. A convexity input, not a signature. Present over $\mathbb{Z}$.
2. **S5 (degree bound).** The positivity consumed is effectivity: zero-multiplicities are non-negative integers, and $\deg(\mathrm{div} f) = 0$. Over $\mathbb{Z}$ this is the valuation/product-formula side (Liouville's inequality), present and load-bearing in all of transcendence theory. Effectivity is ample-cone-side positivity, categorically distinct from the indefinite $(1, n-1)$ intersection signature; the project's own M4 fingerprint (#119-#121: output-indefinite-with-sign-flip) does not match it.
3. **S7 (two-sided conversion).** Over $\mathbb{F}_q$: rationality + FE, both from Riemann-Roch, no positivity beyond $N_n \ge 0$. Over $\mathbb{Z}$: Landau's oscillation theorem for non-negative-coefficient Dirichlet series + the proven FE. The non-negativity used is of the coefficients (the passive comb, Euler-side), which is the already-unconditional half per the acoustic split (#90-94), not the lossless/signature half.
4. **The $q$-square condition and $q > (g+1)^4$.** Bookkeeping about when the optimization has room; removed by the extension-and-descend step. No structural content.

None of the four is a quadratic-form signature; the engine never meets the $(1, n-1)$ object. The two moves that fail, S1 and S4, fail for the same reason: there is no non-identity Frobenius on $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z})$, either globally (the endomorphism) or infinitesimally (the derivation that Frobenius makes cheap). This is exactly the sourcing facet: the repo files the existence of the Frobenius/Tate-module datum under R1 (lever B, #108; [`sourcing_gap_r1.md`](sourcing_gap_r1.md)), and the identity-Frobenius degeneration above is the sharpest elementary form of it.

The prediction held. One refinement: the wall is not R1 alone but R1 twice over (endomorphism + derivation) plus the degenerate self-product; the derivation shortfall and the endomorphism shortfall are the same missing object, since over $\mathbb{F}_q$ one structure ($x \mapsto x^{\sqrt q}$) supplies both. This is consistent with the 2026-07-01 R1 update (R1 as the missing operator behind Frobenius): the counting road's missing ingredient is the operator face, the form roads' missing ingredient is the polarization face, one object, two facets.

## 5. The number-field shadow: what the auxiliary-function corpus actually did over $\mathbb{Z}$

The engine did transfer to number fields; it just transferred the moves that exist there, and its output tracks exactly which moves those are.

### 5.1 Transcendence and Diophantine approximation: S2+S3+S5, with S4 outsourced to $\infty$

Thue-Siegel-Roth, Gelfond-Schneider, Baker: auxiliary polynomial via Siegel lemma (S3), vanishing forced by complex-analytic derivatives at the archimedean place (S4's substitute), contradiction via Liouville/product formula (S5). The theorems are correspondingly about the archimedean place (irrationality measures, transcendence), never about the distribution of primes: the derivative exists only at $\infty$, so the output is about $\infty$. Roth's famous ineffectivity is the S4 shadow: with no Frobenius supplying points to vanish at, the construction needs a hypothetical good approximation to seed it, the same two-case structure that makes Siegel's $L(1,\chi)$ bound ineffective.

### 5.2 Vojta's dictionary and Mordell: the engine plus a genuine surface, still no Frobenius

Vojta (1987 LNM 1239; CIME notes) made the Nevanlinna correspondence precise: Roth = Second Main Theorem without the ramification term; the full SMT's arithmetic analogue is roughly abc; and the missing arithmetic derivative is the named obstruction in the dictionary. Vojta (1991), Faltings (1991), and Bombieri's elementary version (1990) then ran auxiliary functions with Gillet-Soule arithmetic intersection theory on $X \times X$ to prove Mordell. Read structurally: when the curve is a genus $\ge 2$ curve over $\mathbb{Q}$, the product surface exists (Arakelov), S2/S3/S5 run, and the engine proves finiteness of rational points. The one curve on which it cannot be instantiated is $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z})$ itself, whose self-product degenerates and which has no Frobenius correspondence. The same Bombieri who elementarized Weil elementarized Vojta-Faltings; neither elementarization crossed R1.

### 5.3 Bost's slope method

The S2/S3 layer fully geometrized over $\mathbb{Z}$ (Hermitian vector bundles, slope inequalities); used for algebraicity criteria and isogeny bounds, not zeros. It confirms the table's "PRESENT" entries are not vacuous: the arithmetic Riemann-Roch layer is mature, canonical machinery.

### 5.4 Stepanov inside $\mathbb{Z}$: exactly as far as characteristic $p$ reaches

Heath-Brown (1996) and Heath-Brown-Konyagin (2000) bounded Heilbronn's exponential sum $\sum_n e(an^p/p^2)$ by Stepanov's auxiliary-polynomial method, working modulo $p^2$: a thickened point that still carries the $x \mapsto x^p$ structure. The method travels wherever the $p$-power map is an endomorphism-like identity; it stops at the archimedean place and at global $\mathbb{Z}$. (Karatsuba's school similarly used Stepanov's method for character sums, i.e., re-proving Weil-input estimates elementarily; the transfer direction is $\mathbb{F}_q \to \mathbb{Z}$-estimates, never a $\mathbb{Z}$-native engine.)

### 5.5 Contact with the L-zero landscape exists only at the $s = 1$ edge

Baker's linear forms in logarithms (an effective auxiliary-function method) resolved class number 1 and 2, the exceptional-character corner analytic methods could not reach effectively; Granville-Stark (2000) proved uniform abc implies no Siegel zeros for odd real characters. Both live at $s = 1$ / real-character territory, the edge where the Euler product still converges. No result from this corpus reaches inside the critical strip. (Both cited from knowledge; flagged in Section 8.) This edge-only contact is the corpus-level echo of #42/#128: every unconditional auxiliary-function handle lives where the Euler product converges.

### 5.6 The one serious published transfer discussion: van Frankenhuijsen

arXiv:0806.0044 (2008) presents both function-field proofs (Weil's and Stepanov-Bombieri's) side by side and addresses the transfer to $\mathbb{Q}$ directly, stating the obstruction as: $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z}) \times \mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z}) = \mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z})$ is one-dimensional, and proposing that Nevanlinna theory could provide a two-dimensional model carrying a Frobenius action on the geometric coordinate (expanded in his 2014 LMS Student Texts book, not read this session). This is a published, independent localization of the counting road's wall at base + Frobenius, i.e., at R1/PROP, not at a positivity. It also closes the loop with Vojta: the proposed source of the missing structure is exactly the analytic theory whose arithmetic dictionary is missing the derivative.

### 5.7 Buium and Borger: the would-be S4

Buium's arithmetic differential equations build genuine geometry from the Fermat-quotient $p$-derivations $\delta_p(n) = (n - n^p)/p$ (arithmetic jet spaces); Borger reads $\Lambda$-structures as descent to $\mathbb{F}_1$. On $\mathbb{Z}$ itself the structure is trivial (identity Frobenii), and the theory works one prime at a time, $p$-adically, with no archimedean tie-in and no global fixed-point count. No RH attempt exists in this corpus (survey-depth claim).

**The absence finding.** Beyond van Frankenhuijsen's framing chapter, the literature contains no serious attempt to run an auxiliary-function/Stepanov route at RH over $\mathbb{Z}$, and no obstruction theorem either: like R1 itself, this is a gap, not a proven impossibility. The corpus's own summit statements (effective Roth, abc) are conjectures about the missing derivative, i.e., about R1's infinitesimal face.

## 6. Davenport-Heilbronn discipline note

The engine consumes $N = \#C(\mathbb{F}_q)$: the point count is the Euler-product side (the passive comb; fixed points of Frobenius = closed-point data). Davenport-Heilbronn has a functional equation but no Euler product, hence no scheme, no closed points, no Frobenius, no count: the engine cannot even be stated for D-H. This is an Architecture-2-style structural exemption (non-mimicry), not a separator the engine computes. Per the #131 caveat, the exemption is hollow in the marginal-positivity sense: all discriminating power sits in the unbuilt Frobenius, so a hypothetical $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z})$-Stepanov would pass the D-H test by construction, and the test carries no information until S1 is filled. Consistent with S7: the one move with genuine $\mathbb{Z}$-side analytic content (Landau conversion) applies to any FE-symmetric L-function including D-H, which is fine because S7 alone proves nothing; the RH content lives in S1/S4, which are Euler-gated.

1. **A non-polarization road exists in proof-space.** Over $\mathbb{F}_q$, Stepanov-Bombieri proves the RH with no signature anywhere. The premise of "every road is form-shaped" is false already at the theorem that anchors the whole analogy.
2. **Over $\mathbb{Z}$ it dies at R1 (+ the product base), not at M4.** The prediction held. The moves that fail are exactly the two Frobenius-powered ones; the moves that survive (arithmetic Riemann-Roch, Siegel lemma, product formula, Landau conversion) are precisely the ones transcendence theory has been running for a century, with outputs about the archimedean place because the derivative only exists there.
3. **Scope correction to the slogan.** "All roads lead to the signature" is sampled over form-shaped roads. The honest statement, which the repo's own later documents (#130, #131) already carry implicitly: **all roads lead to the universal gap, which has two facets; form-shaped roads wall at M4 (polarization), counting-shaped roads wall at R1 (sourcing/Frobenius)**. This partially confirms #133's estimate that the convergence is ~80% law / ~20% one-axiom artifact: the "signature" phrasing is the artifact part; the one-missing-object reading (R1 and M4 as two faces of the operator behind Frobenius, per the 2026-07-01 R1 update) is the law part, and this audit strengthens it from an independent proof engine.
4. **Coordinates gained (negatives as position).** (a) Do not spend effort elementarizing over $\mathbb{Z}$: the elementary route is R1-gated, and R1 is the FLT-adjacent lever-B residual, so "elementary" buys no discount. (b) The number-field auxiliary-function corpus is the already-built S2/S3/S5 layer; anything new must supply S1/S4, nothing else is missing from that side. (c) WATCH item to add alongside the variety-free-purity trigger: a nontrivial Frobenius-like correspondence on (a model of) $\mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z}) \times \mathrm{Spec}(\mathbb{Z})$ with isolated fixed points at the primes; van Frankenhuijsen's Nevanlinna proposal and Buium-Borger jets are the two published pointers at that slot, and Deninger's flow is its continuous form.
